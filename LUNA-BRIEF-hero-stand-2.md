# Luna brief: the HERO STAND, round 2 (orbit rings, no plate, no grid)

Hi Luna! Your three stand layers are in the game and they work. Rudy has looked at it on the
page and wants a different idea: no disc, no grid, nothing that looks like a floor tile. The
hero floats in space, so his stand should be made of space: a pool of nebula light under him
and thin rings of light circling him, like a small planet with its rings. Everything fades to
black at its edges, so it sits in the dark sky behind him without a visible boundary.

This brief has everything you need; the first stand brief is not required.

## The look to aim for
- Think of a hologram of a planet's ring system drawn in the air, with the hero as the planet.
- Below his feet: a soft, dark cloud of nebula light (deep blue-violet), brightest right under
  him, dissolving into black towards the edges. A few tiny stars in it.
- Around him: two thin elliptical rings of white light seen from a low angle (very flat
  ellipses, like Saturn's rings seen almost edge-on). The front half of each ring is brighter,
  the back half fainter (it passes behind him). Tiny bright specks sit on the rings like
  beads; the game makes them travel round.
- Rising from the centre: a faint column of light with a few specks drifting up, fading to
  nothing by the top third of the picture.
- All light WHITE (the game colours it per hero). No metal, no bevel, no grey body, no grid.

## Pictures (black background; the three 1024 x 512 pictures share ONE framing)
Draw the ellipses centred in the picture, the pool and the rings in the same place in every
file, so the game can stack them exactly.

| File | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `hero-stand_pool-v2.webp` (1024 x 512) | ONLY the nebula pool: a soft ellipse of deep blue-violet haze about 860 px wide and 260 px tall, brightest at the centre, fading to pure black over its outer third; 10-15 tiny stars inside it; no edge line anywhere |
| `hero-stand_ring_a-v2.webp` (1024 x 512) | ONLY the outer ring: one thin white ellipse of light about 940 px wide and 300 px tall, 3-4 px thick, brighter along its front (lower) half, fainter along its back (upper) half, 6-8 small bright beads spaced round it; on black |
| `hero-stand_ring_b-v2.webp` (1024 x 512) | ONLY the inner ring: the same again, about 620 px wide and 200 px tall, 2-3 px thick, 4-6 beads, centred on the same point as the outer ring |
| `hero-stand_beam-v2.webp` (1024 x 1024) | ONLY the column: soft white light rising from the bottom centre, about 480 px wide at its base, narrowing and fading to nothing by the top third; 15-20 small specks of light drifting up inside it; nothing at the very bottom edge that looks like a floor |

Why separate files: the game spins the beads round each ring at its own speed, breathes the
pool, and tints each layer in the hero's element colour (Sparks yellow, Cpt. Frost ice blue,
Nyx purple, Ember orange).

## Common mistakes to avoid
- A plate, a disc, a rim, a grid or any solid surface: nothing solid, only light and haze.
- A hard outline anywhere: every edge dissolves into black.
- Colour in the rings or the beam (white only; the pool alone is blue-violet).
- Rings drawn as circles: they must be flat ellipses (about 3 to 1 wide to tall).
