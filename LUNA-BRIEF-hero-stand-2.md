# Luna brief: the HERO STAND, round 2 (a stand that belongs to the world)

Hi Luna! Your three stand layers are in the game and they work. Rudy's note after seeing it
on the page: the disc reads like a hard metal object pasted over the planet wallpaper. He
wants a stand that BLENDS into the dark sky behind the hero: lighter, airier, more
hologram than hardware. Same three layers, same sizes and names with `-v2`.

## The look to aim for
A holographic projector field rather than a metal plate: no bright metal rim, no solid grey
body. Think of light drawn in the air: a faint elliptical ring of light on the ground, a
fine grid that FADES OUT toward the edges (no hard edge anywhere), and a second ring
floating above, thinner. Everything dark glass is nearly black and see-through, so the
wallpaper shows through it. The outer edge of the whole thing fades to pure black (so there
is no visible boundary on any background).

## Common mistakes to avoid
- A metal rim, a bevel, a grey body: nothing solid.
- A hard outline anywhere: every edge fades.
- Colour in the lights (white only; the game colours them per hero).
