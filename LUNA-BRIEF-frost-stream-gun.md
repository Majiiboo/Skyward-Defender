## What this is for
The penguin's third weapon is his rifle. In the game it does not fire bullets: it sprays one
steady STREAM of ice, like a fire hose or a water gun that freezes. You painted that stream
(`frostshot_stream`) and it looks great.

The problem is the gun itself. Today it looks like an army assault rifle: a thin barrel, a
scope, a magazine, a shoulder stock. A player who sees it expects it to fire bullets.
`rifle_now_side.webp` and `rifle_now_top.webp` in this folder show it.

Rudy wants it redesigned so that one look says "this sprays ice". A futuristic ice sprayer,
a freeze gun. Same penguin, same icy style, a new shape.

It is needed twice: once from the SIDE (shown big on the Weapons screen) and once from
straight ABOVE (carried by the penguin in the fight). Both must clearly be the SAME gun.

## The design: what makes it read as a stream gun
Think of a firefighter's hose nozzle crossed with a science-fiction freeze ray. It must have
these four things, and none of the army-rifle parts:

1. **A TANK of glowing liquid frost.** A clear or half-clear canister, the brightest part of
   the gun, filled with swirling ice-blue light, as if it holds the freezing liquid. It sits
   where a rifle would have its magazine and body: the middle of the gun. Big and obvious.
2. **A WIDE NOZZLE instead of a thin barrel.** The front end opens out like a funnel or a
   hose nozzle, with a thick frosted rim, glowing ice-blue inside. Frost and a little mist
   around its mouth. Clearly wider than the tube behind it.
3. **A short fat TUBE** joining the tank to the nozzle, wrapped in two or three cooling coils
   or rings that glow ice-blue. Frozen over, with icicles hanging from it.
4. **A PIPE or HOSE** that runs from the tank to the back or along the top, so it looks like
   liquid is pumped through the gun.

Leave out: the scope, the magazine, the long thin barrel, the skeleton shoulder stock. A simple
chunky grip for a penguin mitten and a short rounded back end are enough.

Overall shape: shorter and fatter than the rifle today, but still clearly a two-handed long
gun (longer than the pistol, slimmer than the launcher). Our own design; never another game's,
and not a copy of any real tool.
