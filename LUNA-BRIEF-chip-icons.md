# Luna brief: chip symbols (15 small pictures)

Hi Luna. This brief stands on its own: you do not need any earlier brief to follow it.

## What these are for

Every weapon in the game now has three small sockets under it. The player plugs a **chip**
into a socket, and the chip gives the weapon a trick: every few shots something extra happens
(the shot bursts, or flies through a bug, or sets it on fire...).

On the screen a chip is a small shape (a triangle, a diamond, a circle or a hexagon) with a
**symbol** in the middle. The game draws the shape and its coloured edge. **You paint only the
symbol that goes inside.** The reference picture `chip_drawer_ref.png` in this folder shows the
screen as it is today with borrowed symbols; yours replace them.

## The rules, the same for all 15 pictures

- Each picture is **1024 x 1024**, saved as **.webp**, with the exact file name given below.
- **Plain pure black background.** Nothing else in the picture: no frame, no shape round the
  symbol, no text, no letters, no numbers, no shadow on the ground.
- **One symbol, in the middle**, filling about 70% of the picture, with empty black all round.
- **Painted in white and very pale blue-white light only.** No other colours. The game colours
  each symbol itself (grey, green, purple, gold or red, by how rare the chip is), and that only
  works if the symbol is white light on black.
- **Style: a clean glowing emblem**, like a badge on a sci-fi control panel. Bold, simple, solid
  shapes with a soft glow at the edges. It will be shown very small (about the size of a
  fingernail on a phone), so: thick lines, no fine detail, readable as a silhouette.
- All 15 in the same style, the same line thickness, the same amount of glow, as if they came
  from one set.
- A symbol that has a direction points **up**.

## The 15 pictures

For each: the file name, the chip's name in the game, what the chip does, and what to paint.

1. **chip_frag.webp** (Frag: the shot bursts and hurts the bugs around it)
   A small solid ball in the middle with six short thick wedges flying outward from it, evenly
   spaced, like a grenade the instant it bursts.

2. **chip_drill.webp** (Drill: the shot flies on through every bug in its way)
   A thick arrow pointing up that passes straight through a ring: the ring is seen flat-on,
   the arrow's shaft crosses it, the arrowhead is above it.

3. **chip_cryo.webp** (Cryo: the shot slows the bug it hits)
   A bold six-armed snowflake. Thick arms, one small branch pair on each arm, no lace detail.

4. **chip_fire.webp** (Incendiary: the shot sets the bug burning)
   One bold flame, teardrop-shaped with a curl at its tip, and a smaller flame shape cut out
   inside it.

5. **chip_sure.webp** (Sure Shot: the shot is a sure critical hit)
   A crosshair: a thick ring with four short thick marks pointing inward (top, bottom, left,
   right) and a solid dot dead centre.

6. **chip_arc.webp** (Arc: the shot jumps on to a second bug)
   Two solid dots, one lower left and one upper right, joined by a single thick zigzag
   lightning bolt.

7. **chip_guard.webp** (Guard: the shot mends a little of the base's shield)
   A bold shield shape (flat top, pointed bottom) with a thick plus sign cut out of its middle.

8. **chip_bounty.webp** (Bounty: the shot knocks coins out of the bug)
   Three round coins in a loose stack, seen slightly from above, the top one showing a simple
   star stamped on it.

9. **chip_split.webp** (Splitter: the shot brings two more shots beside it)
   Three thick arrows starting from one point at the bottom and fanning upward: one straight
   up, one up-left, one up-right.

10. **chip_leech.webp** (Leech: the shot gives the hero a little health back)
    A bold heart with a thick drop falling into it from above.

11. **chip_mark.webp** (Tracer: the shot marks the bug so every hit on it is critical)
    A diamond-shaped target marker: four thick corner brackets forming a diamond, with a small
    solid diamond in the centre.

12. **chip_over.webp** (Overload: the shot hits three times as hard)
    A thick lightning bolt pointing down inside a broken ring (the ring has three gaps), as if
    the power is too much for it.

13. **chip_stun.webp** (Shock: the shot stops the bug dead for a moment)
    A solid five-pointed star with three short curved lines above it, the way a cartoon shows
    someone seeing stars.

14. **chip_reaper.webp** (Reaper: the shot finishes a bug that is nearly dead)
    A curved scythe blade with a short straight handle, the blade's point toward the upper
    left. Just the blade and handle, no figure.

15. **chip_core.webp** (the weapon's own chip, used for every weapon's built-in chips)
    A hexagonal nut seen flat-on: a thick hexagon ring with a solid round core in the middle
    and three short thick lines running from the core to three of the hexagon's corners, like
    a circuit.

## Checklist before sending

- 15 files, named exactly as above, all 1024 x 1024 .webp.
- Pure black background, white light only, no text, no frames.
- Each symbol readable when you shrink the picture to the size of a postage stamp.

Thank you!
