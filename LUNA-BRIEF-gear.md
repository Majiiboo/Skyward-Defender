# Luna brief: gear icons (18) + chest orbs (3)

Hi Luna! The Gear screen is getting real gear: six slots, three pieces each.
Could you paint one sheet of item icons, plus three small orbs?

## How to lay it out
- One sheet, 6 rows x 3 columns. Row = slot, column = piece (left = basic, middle =
  better, right = best).
- Every cell the same size (512 x 512 is perfect), plain black background, item
  centred, nothing cropped, a little space around it.
- Same painted style and lighting as the pilot and the nav icons. Items alone, no
  pilot wearing them. Three-quarter view, like the turret paintings.
- The three pieces in a row should look like different models (different shapes),
  not the same item recoloured. Colour gets richer left to right:
  basic = charcoal + cyan, better = charcoal + teal/blue, best = charcoal + purple or ember.

## The 18 pieces
| Row | Slot | Basic (left) | Better (middle) | Best (right) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weapon | Laser Pistol: compact one-hand blaster, single cyan barrel | Dual Minis: two small blasters side by side, twin barrels | Pulse Cannon: chunky two-hand cannon, wide glowing muzzle |
| 2 | Helmet (with visor) | Scout Visor: light helmet, open slim visor strip | Warden Helm: full-face helmet, wide visor | Ember Crest: helmet with a raised crest and a glowing V visor |
| 3 | Chest (armour + power orb) | Plate with a ROUND orb in the middle | Layered plate with a TRIANGLE orb | Heavy sculpted plate with a HEXAGON orb |
| 4 | Gauntlets | Grip Gauntlets: plain armoured gloves | Teal Gauntlets: plated, glowing knuckle lines | Surge Gauntlets: heavy, with small vents on the forearm |
| 5 | Boots | Hover Boots: simple armoured boots | Glide Boots: sleeker, a glow strip on the sole | Strider Boots: tall, with small fins at the heel |
| 6 | Jetpack | Ion Pack: one round nozzle | Plasma Pack: two narrow nozzles | Gravity Pack: no nozzle, a dark ring/core where the flame would be |

## Extra small cells: the three chest orbs on their own
One more short row of 3 cells: just the orbs (round, triangle, hexagon), seen straight
from the front, same colours as their chest pieces, about a third of the cell, plain
black background. The game will place them on the pilot's chest and make them glow,
so he shows which chest he's wearing.
