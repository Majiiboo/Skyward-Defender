# Ideas: where Skybound Defender could go next (10 November 2026)

Nothing here is decided. Each idea says what it is, why it would help, and what it needs.
"No art" means I can build it today from what Luna has already painted.

## Built today from this list
- **Kill streak.** Kills in quick succession build a streak; each point is +1% coins from the bugs
  you kill, up to +50%. Getting hit ends it.
- **Planet hazards.** Every planet after the first has its own danger: falling scrap, eruptions,
  shard rain, lightning, a targeting laser. A circle lights up near the hero and then lands. It
  hurts bugs too, so you can lead them into it.

- **Supply drops, a wingmate, the weekly event** (5, 6 and 9 below), then **boss weak points** (3) and
  the **left-handed switch** (19).

The bigger moves (a run across a map, building the base in the fight, a squad of three) are in
IDEAS-overhaul.md.

## Make each planet its own place (the biggest gap today)
1. **A look for each hazard.** The hazards are a coloured circle now. Luna paints one effect per
   planet (a chunk of hull falling, a lava plume, a crystal shard, a bolt, a red beam). Needs art.
2. **A second hazard kind per planet.** Lava lanes that burn a strip for a few seconds, a crystal
   fog that shortens bolts, a magnetic storm that drags pickups sideways. No art for a first pass.
3. **Planet bosses with weak points.** A boss part that opens for two seconds and takes triple
   damage. The Hive Mother already has a "laying" window; give every boss one. No art.
4. **Planet music and ambience.** There is no music at all yet. One loop per planet plus one for
   menus would change how finished the game feels more than any screen. Needs audio.

## More to decide inside a fight
5. **Supply drops.** Every third wave a crate comes down: shoot it open and pick one of two (mend
   the shield, an extra card refresh, or a "cursed" card: big bonus with a drawback). No art.
6. **A wingmate.** Pick a second hero you own as support: he does not fly, he lends his passive at
   half strength and his ability on a long cooldown. Makes owning six heroes matter. No art.
7. **The base super weapon** (already in your queue): the dome fires on a cooldown. Luna's domes
   exist; needs effect art and a button.
8. **Ability upgrades.** Each hero's ability gets three upgrades bought with his shards (wider,
   longer, a second charge). No art.

## Reasons to come back
9. **A weekly event with a theme.** One week "Scrap Rush" (machines only, double barrels), the
   next "Night Hunt" (dark stages, crit twists). A themed track of ten rewards. Reuses twists,
   the Daily Challenge and the Store's Exchange tab. No art for a first one.
10. **A season pass.** Thirty days, a free row and a paid row, fed by everything you already do.
    Needs billing for the paid row; the free row can ship first.
11. **Expedition.** A branching map of ten nodes (fight, elite fight, supply, shop, boss) with one
    hero and no retries; cards carry between fights. The roguelike mode. No art for a first pass.
12. **Hero stories.** Three short chapters per hero, opened by his level: two lines of talk and one
    special fight each. Gives the penguin and the dragon-kin a reason to exist. Portraits exist.
13. **Medals per stage.** Besides stars: Untouched, Speedrun, No AUTO, Streak 50. Shown on the
    Stage Map. No art.
14. **Rankings** for the Daily Challenge and Horde. Needs a server (your 2.0).

## Collecting and showing off
15. **Skins** for heroes, turrets and the base deck (already in your queue; Rook's Ceremonial set
    exists). Needs art per skin.
16. **A hero showcase** on your profile: pick three heroes to stand on Home's horizon. No art.
17. **Titles.** Earned names under your profile ("Hive Breaker", "Untouched"). No art.

## Polish that makes it feel finished
18. **Music and more sound** (see 4). The single biggest polish item.
19. **A left-handed switch** that mirrors the AUTO and ability buttons. No art.
20. **Haptics** (a short buzz on hits, kills and Ascend). No art.
21. **A first-ten-minutes pass**: play a fresh save and smooth every step until stage 5. No art.
22. **Delete the old look** from the code once you are happy with the new one on your phone.

## My pick for the next three
Supply drops (5), a wingmate (6), and the weekly event (9). All three need no art, each changes
what a session feels like, and together they use the heroes, twists and Store that already exist.
