# Luna brief: Captain Frost's weapons SEEN FROM ABOVE (2 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
Your penguin pieces are in the game and Rudy loves how he moves. One thing still looks wrong:
his weapons. He is seen from straight above, but the weapon pictures we have are side views,
so a weapon looks like a sticker glued to his hand.

The fix is to paint each weapon from straight above too, the same way you painted him. Then
the game can tuck it under his arm, with part of it hidden under his body and helmet, the way
a real one would be carried.

You already did this once without knowing: `frostfight_rifle` (the thin icy rifle seen from
above). It is in the game now, tucked under his arm, and it looks right. `rifle_in_game.png`
in this folder shows it. I need the same for his other two weapons.

## Open these first (in this folder)
- `frostfight_rifle.webp` = THE STYLE TO MATCH. Same camera (straight above), same frozen
  metal, same icicles, same thin ice-blue glowing lines.
- `frostfight_whole.webp` = the penguin, so you can judge sizes.
- `rifle_in_game.png` = how the rifle sits on him in the game.

## Rules for both pictures
- 1024 x 1024, plain BLACK background, no hand, no penguin, no shadow, no text.
- Camera straight ABOVE the weapon, looking down on its top. The muzzle (the end the shot
  comes out of) points to the TOP of the picture. The weapon is perfectly upright, not tilted.
- The weapon ALONE in the middle of the picture.
- SIZE: paint it at the size it would be next to the penguin in `frostfight_whole` (his
  helmet is about 330 wide there). The exact lengths are given below. Do not enlarge it to
  fill the picture.
- From above a weapon is narrow; that is correct. Give it a clear shape from above anyway:
  a wider body, a narrow barrel, and something on top (a sight, a rail, a light) so it does
  not read as a plain stick.
- Same family as the rifle: frozen pale metal, frost crust, a few icicles, thin glowing
  ice-blue lines. Our own design; never another game's.

## Picture 1: his PISTOL from above (`frostfight_top_pistol`)
A short, chunky ice pistol.
- About 190 long and about 70 wide (the rifle is about 470 long).
- From the top of the picture down: a short thick barrel with a round glowing ice-blue
  muzzle ring; a wider blocky body with a small sight on top and one glowing line along it;
  the back end rounded, where his mitten will cover it.
- A few short icicles on the sides, fewer and shorter than on the rifle.
The game uses this picture once for his pistol, and TWICE (one in each mitten, the second
mirrored) when he carries two pistols. So keep it symmetrical left to right.

## Picture 2: his LAUNCHER from above (`frostfight_top_launcher`)
A big, heavy ice cannon that rides on his shoulder.
- About 420 long and about 150 wide: clearly fatter than the rifle, a little shorter.
- From the top of the picture down: a wide open muzzle, round, glowing ice-blue inside,
  with a thick frosted rim; a fat barrel like a drum, with two or three glowing rings
  around it; a boxy back end with a vent or two small exhaust ports.
- On top, along the middle, a carrying rail or sight so the top is not plain.
- Thick frost and a few big icicles hanging off the sides.
Keep it symmetrical left to right as well.
