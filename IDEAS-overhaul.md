# Bigger moves: what a major overhaul could be (10 November 2026)

You said you are thinking about an overhaul. These are the big directions I can see, with what each
would change, what it would cost, and what I honestly think. Nothing here is started.

## What I would NOT overhaul

The core of a fight (a hero over a base, drag to fly, bugs come down, cards build up) works, and
after today it finally asks something of the player. The heroes, the elements, the Store and the
clean look are all sound. An overhaul should change how a SESSION is shaped around that core, not
replace it. Replacing the core means throwing away Luna's art and months of tuning.

## Six directions

### 1. The run becomes the game (my first pick)
Today you pick a stage, play it, go back to Home. Sixty stages in a row feel alike.
**Change:** the main mode becomes a RUN of 8 to 10 fights on a small branching map: fight, elite
fight, supply stop, shop, rest, boss. Cards, the hero's health and the base carry from fight to
fight; you choose your path. Lose and the run ends; you keep coins, shards and progress on the
planet. Each planet is one map; beating its boss opens the next.
**Why:** this is what makes people say "one more run". Every choice matters longer, and the same
planet plays differently each time. The campaign's 60 stages become the pool the fights come from.
**Cost:** about a week of my work. No new art. The stage map page becomes the run map.
**Risk:** stage stars and the Stage Map as they are today go away or become medals.

### 2. Build the base during the fight (my second pick)
Today turrets are chosen before the fight and just sit there.
**Change:** each run starts with empty mounts. Between waves you spend scrap picked up in the fight
to place a turret, level it, or swap it, from a hand of three offered (like cards). Your owned
turrets and their levels decide what can be offered and how strong it starts.
**Why:** it turns the base from scenery into the second half of the game, and gives the hull a
story in every fight. It also makes all twelve turrets matter, not the best four.
**Cost:** four or five days. No new art.
**Risk:** more to learn; it must open gently (one mount at stage 5, as today).

### 3. A squad of three, tag in and out
Today one hero flies and one rides along as a wingmate.
**Change:** you bring three heroes. One flies; tap a portrait to swap, with a short cooldown. Each
has his own health bar; a downed hero is out until the next supply stop. Swapping in triggers a
small entry move.
**Why:** it makes the roster the centre of the game and gives elements a real use mid-fight (swap
to the hero the planet is weak to). It is also what a hero-collector game sells.
**Cost:** about a week. No new art (every hero already has his frames).
**Risk:** screen space for three portraits; harder to balance.

### 4. A story campaign you walk through
**Change:** the planets become chapters with a short scene before and after each boss, the
commander and the heroes talking, and one special fight per chapter (escort a convoy, hold for
three minutes, hunt a boss that runs).
**Why:** it gives a reason to see the next planet beyond bigger numbers, and it gives the heroes
voices.
**Cost:** three or four days of my work plus portraits and a few scene pictures from Luna.
**Risk:** writing has to be good and short, or people skip it.

### 5. Play with other people (needs a server)
**Change:** a weekly world boss everyone chips at, friends' heroes as wingmates, rankings for the
Daily Challenge and Horde, gifts between friends.
**Why:** nothing keeps people coming back like other people.
**Cost:** a server and accounts: this is your "2.0". Weeks, and running costs.
**Risk:** the biggest one. Do it after the game is fun alone.

### 6. A new shape for the screen
**Change:** the hero flies the whole screen and bugs come from every side (a survivor-style arena)
instead of down toward a base.
**Why:** it is the most popular shape in the genre right now.
**Cost:** it replaces the core: new enemy moves, no base, turrets rethought. A month or more.
**Risk:** it stops being this game. I would not.

## If you want one sentence
Do 1 and 2 together: a run across a small map where you also build the base as you go. It keeps
everything you have, needs no new art, and changes how the game feels more than any single screen.

## Smaller ideas that keep coming (pick any)
- **Ability upgrades:** three upgrades per hero ability, bought with his shards.
- **Stage medals:** Untouched, Speedrun, No AUTO, Streak 50, shown on the map.
- **Elite as mutations:** each Elite stage adds one named mutation (bugs split, bugs explode,
  bugs regenerate) instead of only more health.
- **A boss rush** mode: every boss you have met, back to back, one life.
- **Convoy fights:** a friendly ship crosses the sky; keep it alive for a bonus.
- **Gem magnet pulse:** a short tap on the hero pulls every pickup in; a thing to time.
- **Hero bonds:** two specific heroes together as flyer and wingmate unlock a small shared bonus.
- **A "last stand":** when the hull breaks, ten seconds of slow motion with triple fire rate to
  win it back.
- **Titles and a profile showcase.**
- **Haptics** on hits, kills and Ascend.
- **Music.** Still the single biggest gap in how finished it feels; I cannot make it.
