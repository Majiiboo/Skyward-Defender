# Luna brief: 6 turret power card pictures (complete, standalone)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; you don't need any earlier brief or message.

## What this is for
The game is a mobile shooter. During a battle the player picks **power cards**. Each card has a picture in the middle of a card frame. Six new cards make the base **turrets** stronger, and they need pictures.

## Match this picture of yours exactly in style
`STYLE_frost_rounds.webp` (in the zip Rudy sends) is your Frost Rounds card picture: a single glowing 3D object, centred, on pure black, with sparks and glow around it. Make the 6 new pictures look like they belong next to it.

## Rules for every picture
- **Size:** 1600 × 1600 pixels, square.
- **Background:** pure black. No frame, no card border, no text, no numbers.
- **Subject:** ONE clear object or effect, centred, filling about 60–70% of the picture. The glow fades into black before the edges; nothing touches the edge.
- **Look:** 3D rendered like your Frost Rounds picture: real metal and glass, bright glowing energy, small sparks and particles. No brush strokes, no outlines.
- **It must read when small:** the card picture shows at about 100 pixels on a phone, so one big bold shape works better than many small details.
- **Files:** one picture per card, named as below.

## The 6 pictures

### 1. `card_ricochet.webp`: Ricochet (Sentry turret)
**What the card does:** the Sentry turret's bolts bounce from one bug to the next.
**Picture:** one bright **cyan energy bolt** zig-zagging through the air, bouncing off three points. At each bounce point, a small burst of cyan sparks. The bolt's path makes a clear "Z" or lightning-like zig-zag of light from the lower left to the upper right.
**Colours:** cyan and white.

### 2. `card_focused_lance.webp`: Focused Lance (Lance turret)
**What the card does:** the Lance's beam gets wider and stronger, and later fires twice.
**Picture:** a **thick, straight spear of white-blue light** pointing straight up, with a bright pointed tip, and **a second thinner beam right beside it** (twin beams). Rings of light around the base of the beams, as if just fired.
**Colours:** white, pale blue.

### 3. `card_chain_storm.webp`: Chain Storm (Storm turret)
**What the card does:** the Storm's lightning jumps between more bugs, and can stun them.
**Picture:** a **small dark storm cloud** at the top, with **purple-violet lightning** jumping down and linking **four small glowing orbs** in a chain (the lightning goes orb → orb → orb).
**Colours:** violet, purple, white.

### 4. `card_event_horizon.webp`: Event Horizon (Gravity turret)
**What the card does:** the Gravity turret pulls from further away, and later swallows enemy shots.
**Picture:** a **swirling black hole**: a pure black centre with a **bright purple ring** around it and a **spiral of purple light** being sucked in. A few small glowing pink-red dots (enemy shots) are being pulled into the spiral.
**Colours:** purple, magenta, black centre.

### 5. `card_hive_surge.webp`: Hive Surge (Swarm turret)
**What the card does:** the Swarm launches more drones, and later they explode on impact.
**Picture:** a **cluster of 6–8 tiny round metal drones**, each with a cyan light, flying outward in a burst from the centre, each leaving a short cyan light trail. One or two of them are bursting into small explosions.
**Colours:** dark metal, cyan lights, orange-white explosions.

### 6. `card_wide_prism.webp`: Wide Prism (Prism turret)
**What the card does:** the Prism splits its shot into more beams, and later the beams slow bugs.
**Picture:** a **clear glass prism crystal** (a triangular crystal) at the bottom, with **one beam of light going in** from below and **a wide fan of 6–7 beams spreading out** from its top, like a rainbow fan, in **warm gold-white light** with a touch of icy blue at the tips.
**Colours:** gold, white, a little icy blue.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Text, numbers or a card frame in the picture (the game adds the frame).
- Glow cut off at the edge of the picture.
- Several small objects that are hard to read when small.
- A background that isn't pure black.
