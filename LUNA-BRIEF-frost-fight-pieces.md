# Luna brief: Captain Frost for the fight, IN PIECES (9 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
Your style check worked: Rudy loves the chibi penguin. He picked picture A ("the swimmer",
seen from straight above) with ONE change: the backpack from your "alt" picture.

Now I need that penguin as separate PIECES, the way you paint the enemies. The game puts the
pieces back together and moves each one: he leans when he turns, the rifle kicks back when he
fires, his flipper works as he flies, his head turns toward the bugs, and his legs drag
behind him.

- `frostfight_check_a.webp` = THE LOOK AND THE POSE. Same camera (straight above), same

## Two changes from picture A
1. THE BACKPACK. Picture A has a tiny rocket on his back. Replace it with the square pack
   from the alt picture: a chunky square box in the middle of his back, the glowing ice-blue
   square light on it, two short jets at its bottom edge pointing DOWN the picture. It should
   cover about half the width of his back so it still shows when he is tiny.
2. THE RIFLE ARM. In picture A he holds the rifle out to the side like a flag. Bring it in:
   the arm reaches FORWARD (up the picture) beside the helmet, and the rifle points straight
   up the picture, close to the helmet but NOT touching or covering it.

Everything else stays exactly as in picture A.

## Rules for ALL nine pictures
- 1024 x 1024, plain BLACK background, no ground, no shadow, no text.
- Camera straight above, his beak and rifle toward the TOP of the picture, as in picture A.
- EVERY PIECE AT THE SAME SIZE IT HAS ON THE WHOLE PENGUIN. Do not enlarge a small piece to
  fill its picture. A boot is small on him, so it is small in its own picture. This matters
  most: it is what lets me put him back together.
- Each piece ALONE, in the middle of its picture, nothing else in the picture.
- The same light on every piece: from above, with a cool blue rim light.
- "Left" and "right" below always mean the left and right SIDE OF THE PICTURE as you look at
  picture A (the flat flipper is on the LEFT, the rifle is on the RIGHT).
- Where a piece is normally hidden under another piece, PAINT IT ANYWAY, simply and rounded.
  Example: the top of a leg is hidden under his body; paint the leg complete with a round
  top. When the leg swings, that hidden part shows.

## How to check your own work
Put `frostfight_body`, `frostfight_helmet`, the flipper, the arm, the two legs and the rifle
on top of each other in their places. If the result looks like `frostfight_whole`, with every
piece the right size, it is perfect.
