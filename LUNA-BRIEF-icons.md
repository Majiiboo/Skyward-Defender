# Luna brief: ICONS (one sheet, every small symbol the menus use)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
The menus use about forty small symbols: currencies, parts, elements, roles, stats, tabs.
Half are painted, half are still simple shapes. One sheet from you replaces all of them
with the same rendered look. Each icon is used small (24 to 48 px on a phone), so shapes
must be bold and simple, readable at thumbnail size, with one strong colour each.

## Look
Glossy 3D objects, like your `icons/coin.png`, `icons/crystal.png` and `icons/chest.png`
(open those three: match their size, shine and framing exactly). Light from above, a soft
rim light. Plain black background. Each icon centred in its cell, filling about 75% of it.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Thin lines or small details: they vanish at 24 px. Bold shapes only.
- Different sizes between cells: every icon about 75% of its cell.
- Labels or text in the picture.
- A tinted background behind an icon (plain black everywhere).
