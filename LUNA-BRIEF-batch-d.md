# Luna brief: batch D (sidekick drone, pilot turn, shots, turrets, gear tiers)

Hi Luna! Rudy played up to stage 13. The things below can't be done well in code alone and need your painting.

Same rules as always:
- Plain black background, one object per cell, nothing cropped at the edges.
- No text.
- Same 3D style and light as the pilot and the base.
- Our own designs.

The sections are in order of importance. Each one is used as soon as it arrives.

## 1. Pilot turn-around (most important)
Today, when the pilot switches from aiming left to aiming right, the game just mirrors him, and it looks like an instant flip. To make it a real turn, the game needs him facing the CAMERA, painted as the same kind of parts as round 1, so the rig can rotate through left 3/4 → front → right 3/4.

Front-facing parts, one sheet, the same scale as round 1:
- helmet with visor
- torso, with the orb socket centred
- hips
- both upper arms
- both forearms with hands (one holding the rifle grip, one open)
- both thighs
- one shin + boot (it will be mirrored)
- both shoulder pads

Also, if possible:
- the rifle seen from the front, pointing up
- the jetpack peeking out behind his shoulders (two small bits on each side)

## 2. Sidekick drone (a new Gear feature)
The drone is leaving the power cards. It becomes a companion you unlock later, upgrade, and customise in Gear. Please paint:
- **3 drone bodies,** each a different silhouette, about the pilot's helmet size:
  - **Scout:** small and round, with 2 rotors.
  - **Striker:** an angular delta shape with 4 rotors.
  - **Warden:** a heavy hexagon with a shield ring.
- **3 weapon pods** that clip under any body: twin blaster, beam emitter, missile rack.
- **One firing flash** for each pod, as light on black.
- **Rotor blades** as a separate piece, so they can spin.

## 3. Muzzle flash (the pilot's rifle)
The flash is a plain coloured circle today. Please paint 4 frames of a cyan muzzle flash, side view, pointing RIGHT, as light on black (it gets added as light):
- a small star burst
- a big cone
- a fading cone
- smoke-free sparks

## 4. Bolt designs for the power cards
Some cards will change how the pilot's bolts LOOK. Paint each one pointing RIGHT, as light on black, 512 x 128:
- **Rail shot:** a long white-cyan needle with a ring around it (Railstorm).
- **Spark bolt:** a cyan bolt with small lightning crackling off it (Chain Spark).
- **Heavy bolt:** a fat plasma slug with a dark core and a bright rim (Heavy Bolts).
- **Split bolt:** two bolts twisting round each other (Burst Fire).

## 5. Turret heads as separate pieces
The Sentry already comes in two pieces (base ring + gun head) and turns to aim. The others are single pictures, so they can only fire straight up. Please split each one into a **BASE** (what plugs into the socket) and a **HEAD** (the part that turns), same style and same size as now:
- Lance Pylon
- Storm Spire
- Gravity Anchor
- Prism Tower

The Swarm Hive can stay as one piece.

## 6. Gear that looks stronger when upgraded
Upgrading a gear piece should change its look. As a test, please paint the **Ion Core Plate** (cyan chest) three times:
- **Tier 1:** as it is now.
- **Tier 2:** extra armour plates and brighter light lines.
- **Tier 3:** gold trim, a glowing orb, small floating energy shards.

If Rudy likes it, we'll do the other pieces the same way.
