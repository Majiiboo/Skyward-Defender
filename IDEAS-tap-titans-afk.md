# Ideas from Rudy's recordings, 2026-11-06: Tap Titans 2 and AFK Arena

Nine minutes of Tap Titans 2 and ten of AFK Arena, both from their first minutes. Neither
plays like ours; both are famous for KEEPING players: something new opens every few minutes,
every number you touch goes up, and the game pays you for coming back. Systems only, never
their art, names or tone. Frames sampled every ten seconds.

## Tap Titans 2 (an idle tapper: the whole game is numbers going up)
What it does in the first ten minutes
- ONE screen for everything: the fight on top, a tabbed tray at the bottom (Sword Master,
  Heroes, Equipment, Pets, Skill Tree, Shop). You never leave the fight to upgrade; every tap
  on "Level Up" shows the DPS it adds ("+1.2 DPS", "+4.5K DPS") next to the price.
- A stage counter with an enemy counter ("8/10", then a boss with a timer), and a floating
  GOAL CHIP at the top of the fight: "Collect 30 gold to hire a hero!", "Attack 20 times!",
  "Reach stage 12!", "Defeat the Stage 8 boss to unlock your first Pet!". The next unlock is
  always named, with a progress fraction (11/12).
- Unlocks in the first ten minutes: a hero, spells, Pets, Equipment, the Skill Tree (its
  branches say "Unlocks at Stage 8000"), Prestige with "Relic Boosters". Each arrives with a
  full-screen "New Pet Unlocked!" card and a tutorial hand.
- Prestige: restart with a permanent bonus (relics). The long game is a loop, not a line.
- Shop: bundles with "x4 value" ribbons, a daily "watch a video for rewards", three chests
  with a daily free claim, diamonds at $2.79 to $12.99, and "progress bundles" that unlock
  by reaching a stage.

## AFK Arena (a hero collector: the roster is the game)
What it does in the first ten minutes
- An illustrated story opening (four painted panels, skippable), then a campaign map with a
  winding road and the player's spot on it; a scripted first fight; a sign-in and a gift
  chest after the first stage.
- AFK REWARDS: a chest at the bottom of the campaign map fills while you are away (gold,
  dust, XP shown per hour); you collect it every visit.
- Heroes: a roster by faction with a filter row, a hero page with a big portrait, a LEVEL UP
  button you can HOLD to level continuously, gear slots round the portrait, a story and a
  skills tab; a NEW HERO splash with the hero's full illustration and three stats; a
  "Team is full" line when the formation is set.
- Quests tab with Daily / Weekly / Campaign lists, each row a progress bar and a Go button
  that jumps to the screen that completes it. The Campaign list is the unlock schedule
  ("Complete stage 2-12 to unlock the Arena").
- Battles auto-play with an "x2" button and an auto-advance ("next stage starts in 5 s").
- A "Perks Bonanza" event card and a "Legendary chest" tap-to-reveal, both in the first
  session.

## What works for them that we already have
Sign-in calendar, daily / weekly / repeat quests with Go buttons, the Patrol (their AFK
chest), the stage map with the player's spot, the hero roster with portraits, red dots, the
NEW pop-up for a freshly opened system, chests with a daily free open, the star map.

## Worth taking (ranked)
1. THE NEXT GOAL, ALWAYS NAMED. Tap Titans keeps one line on screen: what to do next and how
   far along you are ("Reach stage 12: 11/12", "Defeat the stage 15 boss to unlock the
   Wrecker's drop"). We have Unlocks (what opens when) and quests, but nothing on Home says
   the one next thing. A small chip under the stage name: the next unlock or the next
   milestone, with its fraction. Cheap, and it is the single strongest retention device in
   both games.
2. WHAT A LEVEL BUYS, BEFORE YOU BUY IT. "+4.5K DPS" beside every Level Up. Our buttons show
   the price; add the gain ("ATK +3%", "+12 damage per hit") on the same button, and on the
   STATS panel the number that will change.
3. HOLD TO LEVEL. Hold the Level up button to keep levelling (AFK Arena, Tap Titans "Buy
   x1 / x10 / max"). A press-and-hold on our Level up and on turret tracks.
4. THE UNLOCK SPLASH. A new hero, turret or mode gets a full-screen card with its picture
   and three facts, not only a toast. We have the NEW pop-up; make it a card with the art.
5. PRESTIGE as a late loop: "restart planets 1-3 with a permanent +X% bonus" is Tap Titans'
   whole long game. Ours is the red-star ladder and Elite; a prestige would be a 2.0 idea.
6. AUTO-ADVANCE after a win (next stage in 5 s, with a cancel): small, and it keeps a
   session rolling the way AFK Arena does. We have Next on the clear screen.
7. PROGRESS BUNDLES: a shop deal that unlocks by reaching a stage (we have stage packs as
   teasers; their "x4 value" ribbon is the thing people respond to).

Not for us: tapping as the core input, idle gold per second as the economy, the chat bar,
nine systems on one screen at minute three.
