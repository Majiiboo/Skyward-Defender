# Luna: redo list and new cards (after batch D)

Thank you, batch D was great! These are already going into the game:
- the pilot facing the camera
- the three drone bodies
- the turret bases and heads
- the four bolt designs (in use since dev110)
- the muzzle flash (in use; I cropped the barrels off)
- the gear tier test (waiting for Rudy's decision)

Only these need another pass. Same rules: plain black background, no text, our style.

## 1. Drone weapon flashes (redo)
The flashes came with realistic guns painted in and orange fire, so they don't fit.
- Please paint the flashes ONLY, with no gun, as light on black, pointing RIGHT.
- Make them cyan or purple, not orange.
- One flash for each pod:
  - **Twin blaster:** two small cyan bursts side by side.
  - **Beam emitter:** a short purple beam start with a glow ring.
  - **Missile rack:** a small purple puff with sparks.

## 2. Drone weapon pods (redo)
The pods are lovely, but they're seen at an angle and they're bigger than the drones.
- Please paint them **from straight above** (the same view as the drone bodies).
- Make each about a third of the Scout drone's width, so it can clip under any body.
- Same three pods: twin blaster, beam emitter, missile rack.

## 3. New card pictures (512 x 512 each, same style as the other cards)
- **Piercing Rounds:** a bolt passing clean through two small bug shapes in a line.
- **Frost Rounds:** an icy cyan bolt with frost crystals growing off it.
- **Turret Overclock:** the Sentry's gun head with glowing vents and speed lines.
- **Shield Recharge:** the base dome with a ring of cyan light flowing back over it.
- **Thunder Lance** (fusion, so add the pink fusion accent): a long needle bolt with lightning jumping off it.
