# Luna brief: the Solar jetpack (complete, standalone)

Hi Luna! The Arc Welder and the Frost rifle are perfect and already saved for the game. Thank you! This is the last piece. It has been tricky, so this brief tries a different way.

## What keeps going wrong
In every try so far, **Tier 1 is perfect**, but Tiers 2 and 3 turn into a **chest plate**: they grow big shoulder pads at the top corners, a wide collar, and a round orb in the middle of the chest. A jetpack sits on the pilot's BACK, so it must not look like armour for the front.

## Your render to use
`media-generation-gear-fixes2-solar-jetpack-redo-0-670d282d-bd40-4e5a-b1cc-8153ce25d887.webp` (Rudy sends it with this brief). Use ONLY its LEFT piece (Tier 1). Ignore the middle and right pieces.

## The new way to do it
1. **Start from the Tier 1 picture itself and edit it** (image to image), instead of describing a new jetpack from words. Do this once for Tier 2 and once for Tier 3.
2. **When you describe it, call it "a jetpack seen from behind" or "a back-mounted engine pack".** Avoid the words "armour", "plates", "chest", "vest" and "shoulder": they pull the picture towards a chest plate.
3. **If one row of three keeps going wrong, make each tier as its own picture** (512 × 512, pure black, the pack in the middle filling about 70%). Three separate pictures are completely fine; Rudy's game cuts them either way.

## Tier 1 (keep it exactly as it is)
- A pearl-white pack with a flat, narrow top and a rounded bottom.
- ONE big round engine at the bottom, glowing warm gold-white.
- Two small swept-back fins on each side (one higher, one lower).
- Thin gold details and two small vent grilles.

## Tier 2: the SAME pack, a little fancier
Change ONLY these things:
- Thin **gold light lines** glowing in the seams of the white panels.
- A second slim **gold ring** around the engine, and a brighter engine glow.
- The fins a little longer.

Keep the SAME outline as Tier 1. The top stays flat and narrow, and nothing sticks up or out at the top corners.

## Tier 3: the SAME pack as Tier 2, the best version
Add ONLY these things:
- **Polished gold trim** along the edges of the pack and the fins.
- The **brightest** engine glow.
- 4–6 small **warm gold-white crystals** floating around it, inside its square.

The outline is still the same as Tier 1.

## Easy test before sending
Lay a finger over the crystals and the gold: Tier 3 should still look like Tier 1's shape. If you can see shoulder pads, a collar, or a round orb on the front, it's a chest plate. Try again.

## Image rules
- **One row:** 1536 × 512, Tier 1 LEFT, Tier 2 MIDDLE, Tier 3 RIGHT, each in its own 512 × 512 square with clear black space between them. Or three separate 512 × 512 pictures (see step 3 above).
- **Camera:** a 3/4 view from the front-left, slightly from above, the same as Tier 1.
- **Look:** 3D rendered like your last batch. No brush strokes, no outlines.
- **Background:** pure black. No floor, no text.
- **Colours:** pearl white, gold, warm gold-white glow. No cyan.
