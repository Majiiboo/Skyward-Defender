# Ideas from Rudy's recordings, 2026-11-06: Rogue Defense and Undead Slayer

Rudy recorded ten minutes of Rogue Defense (Hybrid Tower Defense) and six of Undead Slayer to
look for elements. Systems only, never their art, names or tone. Frames sampled every few
seconds; numbers read off the HUD.

## Rogue Defense (the close cousin: a dome base at the bottom, enemies falling, cards)
What it does
- One stage is about five minutes: a 20-level run meter ("Lv. 7/20") fills on kills, every
  level is a card pick of three with ONE refresh; some cards carry a "Chain" tag (picking it
  unlocks a follow-up card) and star pips for how many times they were taken.
- Enemies are neon rings and arrows that come in dozens. Small ones die to 2-3 hits
  (-12 to -18 a hit), bigger arrows take -40s; a damage number floats on EVERY hit, so the
  screen is a storm of numbers.
- The boss ("Plague Variant") arrives at 2:00 and lives until about 4:30: HALF the stage is
  the boss, with its bar in 10 layers ("x10") and the waves still coming underneath it. It
  drifts slowly and spits arrow streams; it is the wall.
- Base HP is a NUMBER, 3000, and it drains slowly from leaks (3000 -> 2628 in 4:30) rather
  than in big chunks: the player feels pressure the whole time and never gets one-shot.
- Pause shows a STATISTICS panel: damage and DPS per skill (turret 29k / 265 DPS, beam 17k,
  missile 12k) and the stage loot so far. The clear screen repeats it (turret 318k, beam
  170k) with stars, "Today's attempts 3/3", a Retry, a Rewards x2 (ad) button, and a stage-5
  clear pack for money.
- Home: a 7-day boot camp task list, an SVIP card, a diamond shop, a gear screen with
  Filter / Quick Merge / Loadout, an avatar frame screen.
- Rudy found it hard: the arrows are precise, the numbers are many, and the boss takes long.

Worth taking (ranked)
1. STATS ON PAUSE AND ON THE CLEAR SCREEN: damage and DPS per source (hero, each turret,
   the drone, each card). We already count pilot / turret damage; split it per turret and
   per card and show it. Cheap and it teaches players what works.
2. THE BOSS AS HALF THE STAGE. Their boss arrives at 40% and fights with the waves until the
   end. Ours arrives with the last wave (a third in from planet 3, half on planet 2 since
   dev198). Move it earlier on every planet (wave n-2) and give it more layers (7 on big
   bosses) once the re-tune settles; the boss should be the memory of a stage.
3. CHAIN CARDS: a card tagged "Chain" that unlocks a stronger follow-up when taken. We have
   fusions (two cards make a third); a Chain tag on the cards that lead to a fusion would make
   the system visible.
4. BASE HP AS A NUMBER with slow leaks: our shield / hull bars could show the number and
   enemy contact could chip rather than chunk at the start of a stage (we have hull chunks).
   Try: contact damage halved in the first two waves.
5. "TODAY'S ATTEMPTS 3/3" per stage is their energy; ours is power tokens. Keep ours.
6. Rewards x2 (ad) on the clear screen and a stage-clear money pack: both are in our Store
   plan (ads later, stage packs as teasers).

Not for us: the number storm on every hit (we batch on purpose), the neon-ring look, the
precision bullet hell.

## Undead Slayer (a top-down survivor: not our genre, a few systems worth a look)
- Pick a class first (Warrior / Mage / Hunter) with four skills each: our heroes.
- A 2-minute timed run with a level-up every few seconds: skill cards with rarity colours
  (Common / Rare / Magic) and 5 pips showing levels; REROLL costs coins (50) and SKIP pays
  100: a run's coins spent on picks. AUGMENTATION cards (red) appear now and then: one big
  change to a skill (an extra scythe, +50% size): exactly our overcharge, shown as its own event.
- The HUD shows a row of the skills taken with their level pips.
- Home: sign-in calendar, monthly pass, equipment with durability, a "Durian Grass Land" map.

Worth taking
1. A REROLL THAT COSTS RUN COINS after the free one (we planned ad / crystal refreshes; coins
   picked up in the run are a better, fairer currency for it).
2. The row of taken cards with level pips on the HUD: ours lives behind the cards button; a
   thin row under the top bar would show the build at a glance.
3. The overcharge pick as its own moment (a different frame, a sound), not a third copy of
   the same card.

## Numbers for our balance notes
Rogue Defense: ~5 minutes a stage, boss from 2:00, 10 boss layers, base 3000 HP draining
~12% over a stage, 2-3 hits a small enemy. Ours (dev198 sweep): 1-2 minutes on planet 1,
2.5-4.5 minutes from planet 2, the boss in the last wave, 5 layers, hull never touched by the
bot. The re-tune (16 g) moves toward their pacing without their bullet hell.
