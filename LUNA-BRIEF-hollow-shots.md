# Luna brief: what Hollow's weapons FIRE (9 pictures of VOID LIGHT)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
Your nine ice pictures for the penguin and your fire pictures for Ember are in the game and
they look great (`ice_shots_in_game.png` and `fire_shots_in_game.png` in this folder show them
in a fight). Now the same nine for Hollow, what is left of a pilot, an empty suit filled with purple void, in VOID LIGHT.

Hollow has four weapons and each fires its own kind of shot:

| Weapon | What it fires |
|---|---|
| Pistol | small, quick DARK NEEDLES |
| Twin pistols | TWIN SHADES, small rings of void light |
| Rifle | a steady DRAIN BEAM that pulls life out of bugs |
| Cannon | one BLACK ORB that pulls bugs in and bursts |

Today these are simple shapes drawn by the game's code (`shots_now.png` shows them). Rudy wants
your painting in their place.

The game moves, turns, stretches and scrolls your pictures; you only paint the pieces.

## Open these first (in this folder)
- `ice_shots_in_game.png`, `fire_shots_in_game.png` = your earlier shots in a fight: this is
  how the pieces get used.
- `frostshot_stream.webp`, `frostshot_snowball.webp`, `frostshot_burst.webp`,
  `frostshot_stream_end.webp` = four of your ice pictures. The new ones must be laid out EXACTLY
  the same way (same size, same place in the picture, same black background), only in VOID LIGHT.
- `shots_now.png` = Hollow's four shots today as code shapes. Only to show what each piece is
  for; do not copy the look.
- `hero_reference.webp` and `hero_weapon_side.webp` = Hollow and the rifle, for the colours:
  purple and violet light with a pale lilac-white core.

## Rules for every picture
- 1024 x 1024, plain pure BLACK background, black right up to every edge. No hero, no weapon,
  no text, no frame.
- These are LIGHT: the game adds them on top of the dark sky, so black becomes see-through and
  bright becomes bright. Paint glowing VOID LIGHT: pale lilac-white cores, bright violet bodies, deep purple edges fading to black. Smooth and eerie, not fiery. No dark outlines, no shadows, no grey
  smoke, nothing solid and dark (it would simply not show).
- IMPORTANT for the void: something black cannot be shown, because black is see-through here. So a dark thing is painted as a RING or SHELL of purple light round an empty black middle. The emptiness is the hole in the light.
- Everything FLIES UPWARD: the front of a shot points to the TOP of the picture.
- One thing per picture, dead centre, with clear black space around it (except picture 5).
- The same look across all nine, so they read as one family. Our own design; never another
  game's.

## The nine pictures

### 1. `hollowshot_pellet` : one dark needle
A long, very thin needle of violet light with a bright lilac point at the top and a faint wisp of purple trailing below. About 400 tall and 130 wide.

### 2. `hollowshot_pellet_big` : the charged shot
Every few pistol shots is a stronger one. The same shot, but longer and fiercer:
a thicker needle with a small ring of violet light round its middle and two thin wisps curling off it. About 600 tall and 220 wide.

### 3. `hollowshot_ball` : one shade
A ring of violet light round an empty black middle, like a small eclipse, with thin wisps of purple streaming off behind (below) it. About 420 across.

### 4. `hollowshot_boulder` : the cannon's shot
A black orb: a thick ring of bright violet light round an empty black centre, with streaks of purple light spiralling INTO it from all round, as if it is sucking light in. About 620 across. It must look HEAVY. The game spins it, so no clear top or
bottom; make it roughly round overall.

### 5. `hollowshot_stream` : the drain beam (THE IMPORTANT ONE)
A strip that the game repeats end to end and scrolls, to make one long stream.
Think of a beam that drinks: two or three thin ribbons of violet light twisting round each other up the middle, with small motes of pale light travelling along it. Smooth and flowing.
- It runs straight up the MIDDLE of the picture, about 320 wide, soft on the left and right
  sides (it thins out to black).
- It must touch the TOP edge and the BOTTOM edge of the picture, and it must LOOP: the top
  edge has to join onto the bottom edge with no visible seam, because the game stacks copies
  of this picture one above the other. The easiest way: keep it the same width, brightness
  and texture all the way up, with no start and no end. Your `frostshot_stream` did this
  perfectly.
- No bright blob at either end. Even from top to bottom.

### 6. `hollowshot_stream_end` : where the stream ends
The stream only reaches a short way and then breaks up. Paint that end: it comes in from the
bottom of the picture about 320 wide, the same brightness and texture as picture 5, and
unravels toward the top into separate thin wisps that curl apart and fade, fading to nothing. About 800 tall, 700 wide at its widest. (Like your
`frostshot_stream_end`.)

### 7. `hollowshot_bits` : four small pieces on one picture
Divide the picture into four equal squares (2 across, 2 down), one piece in the middle of
each square, each about 300 across:
- top left: a tiny ring of violet light
- top right: a small curled wisp of purple
- bottom left: a four-pointed spark (a glint of light)
- bottom right: a soft round puff of violet glow
The game throws these around the shots and the hits.

### 8. `hollowshot_hit` : a small hit
What shows for a split second when a shot hits a bug, seen from straight above:
a small ring of violet light that looks as if it is closing inward, with a few wisps being pulled toward the middle. About 600 across. Round, the same in every direction.

### 9. `hollowshot_burst` : the cannon shot's explosion
The cannon's shot bursts when it hits. Seen from straight above: a big round blast, about 900
across. An empty black centre, a thick bright violet ring round it, and streaks of purple light flying outward in every direction. Round, the same in every direction; the game grows it and fades it.
