# Luna brief: three more EVOLUTION emblems

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for

Your 57 card emblems are all in the game and they look right. Three new evolution cards were
added after that list was written, so they have no emblem yet. Please paint these three in
exactly the same style as your other evolution emblems.

In this folder:

- `STYLE_storm_volley.webp`, `STYLE_thunder_lance.webp`: two of your own evolution emblems.
  Same style, same colours, same size in the frame.
- `FROM_shrapnel.webp`, `FROM_overdrive.webp`, `FROM_ricochet.webp`: your emblems for the three cards these evolutions
  grow out of. Each new emblem should look like the grown-up version of its FROM picture.

## The style (the same as before)

- **ONE symbol**, seen straight from the front. Not a scene, no perspective.
- **Thick and solid**, like a chunky enamel pin: bevelled edges, a lit top face, a darker
  underside.
- **Lit from above**, a crisp pale rim, a soft round glow behind it that fades to nothing. No
  ring, no plate, no frame.
- **Evolution colours**: pale gold on the lit faces, gold `#FFD152` melting into pink
  `#FF73CC` in the body, deep plum on the underside.
- The symbol fills about **80%** of the picture. Made of 1 to 5 big shapes; nothing thinner
  than a pencil.
- Plain **BLACK** background, centred, nothing cut off. 1024 x 1024, `.webp`, the exact file
  name below.
- Our own designs; never another game's.

## The three pictures

| File | Card | The symbol |
|---|---|---|
| `pic_cluster_storm.webp` | Cluster Storm: a dead bug bursts into shards that fly after the nearest bugs and burst them too | Look at `FROM_shrapnel`. The same burst, bigger: one thick star-shaped burst in the middle with **four fat shards** flying out of it, and each shard is **curved like a claw**, all bending the same way round (like a pinwheel), so they look like they are hunting, not just flying straight. |
| `pic_afterburner.webp` | Afterburner: while charged up, the hero leaves a trail of fire behind him | Look at `FROM_overdrive`. Keep its fat lightning bolt in the middle, pointing up. Under it, **three thick flame tongues** fanning downward like the exhaust of a rocket, the middle one longest. No ring this time: the bolt and the flames are the whole symbol. |
| `pic_prism_shot.webp` | Prism Shot: a shot that bounces off a bug splits into three | Look at `FROM_ricochet`. One fat bolt coming up from the bottom into a **thick triangle (a prism)** in the middle, and **three fat bolts** leaving the top of it, fanned out like a bird's foot. Five big shapes in all. |
