# Luna brief: two more EVOLUTION emblems

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for

Your 57 card emblems are all in the game and they look right. Two new evolution cards were
added after that list was written, so they have no emblem yet. Please paint these two in
exactly the same style as your other evolution emblems.

In this folder:

- `STYLE_storm_volley.webp`, `STYLE_thunder_lance.webp`: two of your own evolution emblems.
  Same style, same colours, same size in the frame.
- `FROM_shrapnel.webp`, `FROM_overdrive.webp`: your emblems for the two cards these evolutions
  grow out of. Each new emblem should look like the grown-up version of its FROM picture.

## The style (the same as before)

- **ONE symbol**, seen straight from the front. Not a scene, no perspective.
- **Thick and solid**, like a chunky enamel pin: bevelled edges, a lit top face, a darker
  underside.
- **Lit from above**, a crisp pale rim, a soft round glow behind it that fades to nothing. No
  ring, no plate, no frame.
- **Evolution colours**: pale gold on the lit faces, gold `#FFD152` melting into pink
  `#FF73CC` in the body, deep plum on the underside.
- The symbol fills about **80%** of the picture. Made of 1 to 5 big shapes; nothing thinner
  than a pencil.
- Plain **BLACK** background, centred, nothing cut off. 1024 x 1024, `.webp`, the exact file
  name below.
- Our own designs; never another game's.

## The two pictures
