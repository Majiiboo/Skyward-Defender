## What this is for

Your four style-check emblems (Burst Fire, Aegis, Rapid Pulse, Storm Volley) are in the game

Now please paint the rest of the cards the same way. There are 57. Please send them **batch by

need to wait until all are done.

## The style (the same as your four)

Think of a chunky enamel pin, or a symbol cast in thick frosted glass and metal.

- **ONE symbol**, seen straight from the front. Not a scene, not a realistic object, no
  perspective.
- **Thick and solid**: bevelled edges, a lit top face, a darker underside, as thick as a finger.
- **Lit from above**, one bright highlight along the upper edge, a crisp pale rim.
- **A soft round glow behind it** in the same colour, fading to nothing. No hard ring, no plate,
  no frame.
- **One colour family per picture** (given per batch), from nearly white on the lit faces to a
  deep shade on the underside.
- Plain **BLACK** background, centred, nothing cut off. 1024 x 1024, `.webp`, the exact file
  name given.
- Our own designs; never another game's.

## Two small changes from the style check

1. **Bigger and bolder.** On a phone the picture is the size of a thumbnail. Please let the
   symbol fill about **80%** of the picture (your four fill about 65%), and make every part of
   it thick. If a part is thinner than a pencil at full size, it disappears on the phone.
2. **No small marks.** In `STYLE_storm_volley` the little lightning mark between the bolts
   cannot be seen at card size. A symbol should be made of 1 to 5 big shapes, nothing smaller.

## One check before you send each batch

Shrink the picture to the size of your thumbnail. Can you still name the shape in half a
second? Is it thick, lit from above, one colour family, filling most of the frame? Then it is
right. If it looks like a small scene or has thin lines, it is the old style.
