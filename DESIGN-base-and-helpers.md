# Replacing the turrets: weapons, helpers and a base you build (10 November 2026)

Your idea, in your words: scrap the turrets and give the player other kinds of help: better and more
varied weapons, different power-ups, helpers (drones or a sidekick), and a base that you build up so
that it adds defence the more you build it. You asked what I think. Nothing here is built.

## What I think: yes

The turrets are the weakest part of the game, and for reasons that tuning will not fix:

- **They ask nothing of you.** You pick four before the fight and they shoot by themselves. They do a
  quarter of the damage and you never decide anything about them once the fight starts.
- **They do the hero's job, worse.** The hero shoots bugs; the turrets shoot bugs. Two things doing
  the same job make each feel smaller.
- **They have always looked pasted on.** Your own playtest notes say so three times (the Lance beam
  not lining up, bases vanishing, "they throw me off").

Your three kinds of help are better because each answers a DIFFERENT question, so they do not
compete with each other:

| Kind of help | The question it answers | When you decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon | How do I shoot? | Before the fight, and it changes how your hands play |
| Helpers | Who flies with me? | Before the fight; they act on their own |
| The base | What happens when things get past me? | Between sessions: you build it up over weeks |

## The part I like most: the base replaces the turret economy, it does not add one

Last week you asked about building a home base and I advised against it, because it would have been
a second economy on top of turrets. If the turrets go, that objection goes with them. The base
takes the turrets' place: same coins, same parts, same four sockets and the dome, and **Luna's
twelve turret paintings become the buildings**, so no art is thrown away.

A first set of buildings, each on a socket or the dome, each with levels:

| Building | What it does | Art it can wear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shield generator | A bigger shield, and it mends faster between waves | Prism Tower |
| Hull plating | More hull; the base takes less from each hit | the dome itself |
| Point defence | Zaps the few bugs that actually reach the dome (small, automatic) | Sentry |
| Repair bay | Mends the hull a little after every wave | Repair Node |
| Hangar | One more drone slot per two levels | Swarm Hive |
| Reactor | Your hero's ability charges faster | Storm Spire |
| Radar | Runner lanes and hazards warn you earlier | Dread Beacon |
| Refinery | More coins from every fight | Gravity Anchor |

You have four sockets, so you choose four of the eight: that is the decision, and it is a real one
(a defensive base, a helper base, an economy base). The base is also something to show: it changes
on Home as it grows.

## Helpers: a small squad instead of one drone

You already own the pieces: six drone bodies, five pods, and the wingmate I built today.

- **One wingmate** (a second hero): lends half his passive and fires his ability by himself.
- **Up to two drones**, each with a job instead of all being small guns: a gunner, a shield drone
  that blocks a shot now and then, a collector that fetches pickups, a medic that mends the hero.
  The Hangar building decides how many you may bring.
- What the special turrets did moves here: the Tesla Coil's chain becomes an arc drone, the Cryo
  Lens a frost drone, the Mine Layer a drone that drops mines.

## Weapons: fewer, more different

There are six weapons today, one per element, and they mostly differ in numbers.

- Make each one a different way of shooting that you can feel: a fast pistol, a twin, a slow cannon
  with a blast, a rail that pierces a line, a beam you hold on a target, a spread.
- Give each weapon a short path of three evolutions that change its shape, not only its damage.
- Any hero can carry any weapon; his own element's weapon gets a bonus.

## Power-ups

The power cards stay as they are; they are the best system in the game. The twelve turret cards
become cards for drones and buildings (a Sentry card becomes a Point defence card, and so on).

## What it would cost, and in what order

About two weeks of my work, no new art needed for a first version. I would do it in three steps,
each one playable on its own:

1. **The base** (about a week): buildings replace turrets in the fight and on the Turrets page;
   what you spent on turrets carries over to buildings. This is the big one.
2. **The drone squad** (three or four days).
3. **Weapons that play differently** (three or four days).

## What worries me

- **It is a real change for anyone with a save** (today that is only you). Turret levels would turn
  into building levels, so nothing you spent is lost, but the Turrets page as you know it goes.
- **The Wrecker mini-boss grabs a turret.** It would grab a building instead and switch it off
  until shot away, which is arguably better.
- **Less on screen shooting.** Without turrets firing, a fight is quieter until the drone squad is
  in. Steps 1 and 2 should land close together.

## One question for you

Should the Point defence building still shoot (a small automatic gun, the last trace of the
turrets), or should the base be purely defensive, with all the shooting left to the hero and his
helpers? I lean to purely defensive: it makes the split between the three kinds of help cleanest.
