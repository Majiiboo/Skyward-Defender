# Luna brief: 16 gear pieces to re-render (complete, standalone)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need. You don't need any earlier brief or message. Please read it all once, then do the pieces one by one and tick the checklist at the end for each.

## What this is
The game is a mobile shooter. A small chibi armoured pilot flies above a domed base and shoots alien bugs. He wears gear in **6 slots**:
1. Weapon
2. Helmet
3. Chest
4. Gauntlets (both forearms and hands)
5. Boots (both)
6. Jetpack (on his back)

The gear comes in **sets**, and each set has its own colour and look.

Each piece is painted **3 times**, as 3 tiers that show its upgrade level:
- **Tier 1:** the basic piece.
- **Tier 2:** the SAME piece plus 2–3 extra armour plates, with thicker, brighter light lines.
- **Tier 3:** the SAME piece as Tier 2, plus polished gold trim on the edges, the strongest glow, and 4–6 small glowing crystal shards floating around it.

**The shape never changes between tiers.** If you cover Tier 3's gold and crystals, it should look like Tier 2.

## The look (very important)
**3D rendered, like a modern game.** NOT painted, no brush strokes, no drawn outlines.
- **Materials:**
  - Armour is dark matte metal with tiny scratches.
  - Trim is polished metal.
  - Glass has reflections.
- **Details:** bevelled edges, panel seams, small bolts and vents.
- **Light:** from above and slightly in front, with soft shadows in the seams and a thin cool rim light on the outer edges.
- **Glow:** light lines and orbs really glow and tint the metal next to them.
- **Crystals:** glowing gem shards in the SET's colour (see each set below).

## Image rules (every image)
- **ONE image per piece,** showing its 3 tiers in one row: LEFT = Tier 1, MIDDLE = Tier 2, RIGHT = Tier 3.
- **Size:** 1536 × 512 pixels. Imagine three invisible 512 × 512 squares; each tier sits in the middle of its square.
- **Spacing:** each tier fills about 70% of its square. **There must be clear black space between the three tiers. They must NOT overlap or touch.**
- **Background:** pure black. No floor, no shadow on the ground, no text, no numbers.
- **Camera:**
  - Weapons: exactly side-on, pointing RIGHT (barrel on the right, grip at the bottom-left).
  - Everything else: a 3/4 view from the front-left, slightly from above.
- **Pairs:** gauntlets and boots are a pair (left and right) side by side within each tier's square.
- **Jetpacks:** NEVER have shoulder straps, backpack straps or shoulder pads. A jetpack is an armoured box or plate that mounts on the back, with its thrusters or engines at the BOTTOM.
