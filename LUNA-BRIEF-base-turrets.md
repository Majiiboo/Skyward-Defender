# Luna brief: home base, deck and turrets (redo)

Hi Luna! On the battle screen the base and turrets look pasted on: too dark, too small,
and you can barely tell a turret is firing. We want them to feel heavy and important,
like real defences that sit IN the scene. They don't have to match the old charcoal
style; each turret can look completely different.

## The scene they live in
- Portrait phone screen. The base sits along the bottom edge, seen from the front and
  a little above (like looking across a landing pad at the horizon).
- Night sky in deep navy/indigo, a glowing planet horizon behind, a cyan force-field
  dome drawn by the game over the base (don't paint the dome).
- Light comes from above and slightly in front, with a cool cyan rim light from the
  dome. Please give everything a soft contact shadow / floor glow where it touches the
  deck, so it looks planted, not floating.
- Strong, simple shapes and bright light accents so they still read when small.

## Sheet 1: the base (one wide painting)
- One long strip, 2048 x 640, plain black background above it.
- A raised armoured deck across the whole width with a command building in the middle
  (dome or bunker, landing lights, antenna, a shield emitter on top).
- Four turret sockets built into the deck, two each side of the building, evenly
  spaced: round armoured platforms with a lit ring and a small number 1-4. Leave the
  sockets EMPTY (the turrets are separate).
- A second copy of the same strip with every socket's ring lit brighter (used when a
  turret is placed there).

## Sheet 2: the six turrets
- One row per turret, 3 cells each, every cell 512 x 512, plain black background.
- Cells: IDLE, CHARGING (glow building up), FIRING (flash at the weapon, recoil).
- Painted WITHOUT their own floor plate: each turret's bottom should fit into a round
  socket, centred at the bottom of the cell, so the same foot sits in the deck.
- Same camera angle as the base. Big and chunky: they should fill most of the cell.

| Turret | Idea (free to reinterpret) | Main colour |
|---|---|---|
| Sentry | Heavy twin-barrel autocannon in an armoured rotating housing | cyan tracers |
| Lance Pylon | Tall rail obelisk that charges a beam of light straight up | white-blue |
| Storm Spire | Tesla tower with coils and a glowing orb on top | violet lightning |
| Gravity Anchor | Heavy ring generator around a dark singularity core | deep purple |
| Swarm Hive | Armoured drone hangar with hatch doors, drones docked inside | amber + cyan |
| Prism Tower | Big faceted crystal held in a gimbal frame | rainbow |

## Sheet 3: shots (small cells, 256 x 256, plain black)
For each turret, one PROJECTILE and one IMPACT: Sentry tracer round + spark burst,
Lance beam segment + hit flare, Storm lightning bolt piece + zap, Gravity pulse ring +
crush flash, Swarm micro-drone (top view) + pop, Prism beam segment + rainbow burst.
Bright and thick enough to see across the screen.

Thank you!
