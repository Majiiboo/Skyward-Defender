# Luna brief: the pilot's starter suit (complete, standalone)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; you don't need any earlier brief or message. Please read it all once before starting.

## What this is and why
The game's hero is a small chibi armoured pilot. Right now he wears your most detailed armour: dark plates, lots of purple and cyan light lines. That makes him look like a fully upgraded hero on the very first day, even though the player hasn't upgraded anything yet.

So we want a **plainer "starter suit"**: the rookie version of the same pilot. Today's armour isn't thrown away; it becomes a special suit players earn later.

**Same pilot, same body, same chibi shape. Only the outfit is simpler.**

## The three templates (your own renders: copy their layout EXACTLY)
Rudy sends these with this brief. Each new sheet must be a **repaint of its template**: the same picture size, every part in the same cell, the same position in the cell, the same size, the same angle, and the round joint balls in the same places. Only the armour design changes. The game cuts the parts out at fixed spots, so if a part moves, it won't fit.

## The starter suit design (the same rules for every part)
- **Base:** a padded **flight suit** in matte medium grey fabric, with soft folds and seams. You can see it's fabric, not metal.
- **Armour:** only a few **small, plain plates** in darker grey metal (chest, forearms, shins, knees), with simple rounded edges, a few bolts, and light scuffs.
- **Lights:** **cyan only, and very few:** at most ONE short thin cyan light strip per part. **No purple at all.** No glowing panel patterns.
- **Helmet:** a smooth, rounded flight helmet with a plain shell. Keep the visor slit in the SAME shape and place (it still glows cyan). Use a simple mouth guard, no heavy vents, no big ear pieces.
- **Look:** the same 3D-rendered style as the templates: real fabric and metal, soft shadows, a thin cool rim light. No brush strokes, no outlines.
- **The feeling:** clean and capable, a fresh recruit. Not ragged, not plain grey clay. He should still look good, just clearly less armoured than today.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Parts moved, resized or turned compared with the template.
- Missing joint balls (the round balls at the joints are how the parts connect).
- Purple light lines, or lots of glowing panels.
- A rifle anywhere.
- Flames on the jetpack or thrusters.
- Text, labels or a floor.
