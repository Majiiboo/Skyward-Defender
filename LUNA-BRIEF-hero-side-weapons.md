# Luna brief: each hero's own pistol and launcher from the SIDE (11 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for

The game has a Weapon page. The weapon the player is holding is shown big in the middle of the
screen, lying on its side, so people can admire it. `weapon_page_in_game.png` in this folder
shows that page with the penguin's ice rifle.

Every hero carries four weapons: a **pistol**, **two pistols at once** (the game just shows the
pistol twice), a **rifle**, and a big **launcher**. You already painted a rifle from the side for
five of the heroes. Only the penguin has his own pistol and launcher. The other five heroes show
borrowed pictures for those, which look wrong next to their own rifle.

I need each of those five heroes' **pistol** and **launcher**, painted from the side, in the
same style as that hero's rifle. Rook has no side rifle yet, so he gets a third picture.

## The folder

- `weapon_page_in_game.png` = where the pictures go.
- `STYLE_pistol.webp` and `STYLE_launcher.webp` = the penguin's pistol and launcher that you
  painted before. **These two are the camera, the size and the amount of detail to match.**
  Only the theme changes per hero.
- One folder per hero (`rook`, `sparks`, `hollow`, `nyx`, `ember`) with:
  - `reference.webp` (for Rook: `turn_commander_1.webp`) = the hero, for his colours and materials.
  - `weapon_side.webp` = that hero's rifle from the side, as you painted it. His pistol and
    launcher must look like they came from the same workshop as this rifle. (Rook has none.)

## Rules for all 11 pictures

- 1024 x 1024, plain **black** background, no hand, no hero, no shadow, no text.
- A flat **side** view: we see the whole left side of the weapon.
- The weapon stands **upright**: the muzzle (the end the shot comes out of) points to the **top**
  of the picture, the grip points to the **right**. Exactly like the two STYLE pictures.
- The weapon alone, in the middle. A launcher fills about 800 of the picture's height, a rifle
  about 850, a pistol about 600.
- Sharp and detailed: these are shown big.
- The same 3D-rendered look as the heroes and their rifles.
- Our own designs; never another game's.

## What a pistol and a launcher are, for every hero

- **Pistol:** short and chunky. From the top down: a short thick barrel with a glowing muzzle,
  a blocky body with a small sight on its back edge, a thick grip sticking out to the right with
  a trigger guard.
- **Launcher:** big and heavy, carried on the shoulder. From the top down: a wide open muzzle
  with a thick rim, glowing inside; a fat barrel like a drum; a boxy back end with a vent. On the
  right side a curved shoulder rest and a front grip.

Each hero's pair below says how to dress those two shapes in his theme.

## ROOK (the cadet in the charcoal and purple armour; his light is bright cyan)

Clean military science-fiction. Matte charcoal plates, purple trim lines, cyan glowing energy.
Tidy, new, factory-made.

1. **`rook_side_pistol.webp`** A compact service pistol. Charcoal body, one purple stripe along
   it, a cyan glowing ring at the muzzle and a small cyan energy cell showing through a slot in
   the body.
2. **`rook_side_rifle.webp`** A sleek energy carbine, longer and slimmer than the launcher.
   From the top down: a slim barrel with a cyan muzzle ring and three thin cooling fins; a
   charcoal body with a long cyan glowing strip along it and a purple stripe; a short stock at
   the bottom; grip and trigger guard to the right.
3. **`rook_side_launcher.webp`** A shoulder mortar. A wide cyan-glowing muzzle with a charcoal
   rim, a drum barrel with two purple bands, a boxy back end with a cyan vent grille.

## SPARKS (the junkyard robot; his light is electric green)

Built from scrap. Rusty mismatched plates, bolts, exposed wires, patches welded on, green
electric glow. Nothing matches, but it all works.

4. **`sparks_side_pistol.webp`** A rivet gun turned into a pistol. A stubby barrel made from a
   pipe with a green glowing coil wound round it, a body of two different bolted plates, a
   wire looping from the body to the barrel, a grip wrapped in tape.
5. **`sparks_side_launcher.webp`** A junk launcher. The muzzle is a wide dented drum (like an
   oil barrel end) glowing green inside, the barrel is ringed with three mismatched metal hoops,
   thick cables run down its side, the back end is a boxy battery pack with green lights.

## HOLLOW (an empty suit filled with purple void; his light is violet)

Smooth, dark, eerie. Black-violet metal with a soft sheen, no bolts, flowing curves, and
openings that show violet light inside as if the weapon is hollow too.

6. **`hollow_side_pistol.webp`** A smooth dark pistol with no hard edges. The muzzle is a ring
   of violet light round an empty black hole. Two long slots in the body show violet glow
   inside.
7. **`hollow_side_launcher.webp`** A heavy pulse cannon. A wide muzzle that is a thick violet
   glowing ring round blackness, a smooth dark drum barrel with three thin violet rings floating
   just off its surface, a tapered back end with one violet glowing slit.

## NYX (the night huntress; her light is blood red)

Sharp and elegant. Black lacquered metal, blade-like edges, thin crimson glowing lines, a few
small spikes. Like a hunter's finely made tools.

8. **`nyx_side_pistol.webp`** A slim, wicked pistol. A narrow barrel with a blade-like fin along
   its back edge, a crimson glowing line running the length of the body, a muzzle with a small
   red glow, a slender grip with a pointed base.
9. **`nyx_side_launcher.webp`** A dread mortar. A wide muzzle ringed with six short spikes
   like a crown, red glow inside, a black drum barrel with thin crimson lines in a claw-mark
   pattern, a back end shaped like a pointed blade.

## EMBER (the dragon-kin; her light is orange fire)

Forged and hot. Dark bronze and blackened iron, dragon-scale patterns, glowing orange cracks
as if the metal is heated from inside, small horn shapes.

10. **`ember_side_pistol.webp`** A forged bronze pistol. The muzzle is shaped like a small
    dragon's open mouth, glowing orange inside. Scale pattern on the body, two glowing orange
    cracks, a grip of dark leather wrap.
11. **`ember_side_launcher.webp`** A sun mortar. A wide muzzle like a big dragon's open mouth
    with a bronze rim and orange fire-glow inside, a drum barrel covered in overlapping scales
    with orange light between them, two small swept-back horns at the back end.
