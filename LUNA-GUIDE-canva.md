# Luna guide: using Canva to fix your renders (complete, standalone)

Hi Luna! Rudy has Canva Pro, and it can help with the small fixes that are hard to get right by making a whole new render. This guide explains when to use Canva, the four tools to use, and exactly how.

## The one big rule
**Canva is for EDITING your renders, never for making new art.**
- Keep making new pieces in your usual image tool. That's what gives the game one consistent 3D look.
- Do NOT use Canva's "Magic Media" / text-to-image to create gear. Its style is different, and it would look out of place next to your other pieces.
- Use Canva only to erase, repaint a small area, move a piece, or line pieces up on a sheet.

## The four Canva tools to use
Canva moves its menus around sometimes. If a tool isn't where this guide says, type its name into Canva's search box.

| Tool | What it does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Magic Eraser** | Brush over something and it disappears; the background fills in. | Removing a wrong part: shoulder pads, straps, a stray crystal. |
| **Magic Edit** | Brush over an area, type what should be there, and it repaints only that area. | Small changes: recolouring crystals, swapping a gem colour, fixing a glow. |
| **Magic Grab** | Lifts one object out of a picture so you can move or resize it. | Spreading out tiers that touch or overlap. |
| **A blank design** | A canvas you place pictures on. | Building one clean 1536 × 512 sheet from separate pictures. |

**How to open them:** upload your render, put it in a design, click the picture, click **Edit** in the top bar, then look under **Tools** (or **Magic Studio**). Click **See all** if the tool isn't shown.

## Rules for every edit
1. **Edit small areas.** The bigger the area you brush, the more Canva invents, and the less it looks like your render. Brush just the part that's wrong, plus a small margin.
2. **The background must stay PURE black.** After erasing, zoom in: if Canva left a grey smudge or a soft shadow, erase it again.
3. **Never add Canva extras:** no stickers, no text, no frames, no drop shadows, no filters, no "enhance".
4. **Export as PNG at full size:** Share → Download → File type **PNG**, size ×1. Not JPG: JPG makes the edges blurry.
5. **Keep your original render.** Save the edited one under a new name ending in `-canva`, so Rudy can tell which one to use.

## Job 1 (only when Rudy asks): removing a wrong part
For example: "the jetpack has shoulder straps" or "Tier 3 has an extra orb".
1. Make a design the same size as your render (usually **1536 × 512**) and drag the render in so it fills the design exactly.
2. Click the picture → **Edit** → **Magic Eraser**.
3. Brush over ONLY the wrong part, one part at a time, and click **Erase**.
4. Zoom in. If grey smudges are left on the black, erase them too.
5. If a hole is left in the piece itself, use **Magic Edit** on just that hole, and type what the surface around it looks like (for example `smooth pearl white panel with thin gold seam lines`).
6. Download as a full-size PNG, named with `-canva` at the end.

## Job 2 (only when Rudy asks): spreading out tiers that touch
Use this when a sheet's three tiers overlap or touch.
1. Make a **1536 × 512** design with a **pure black** background (click the background → colour **#000000**).
2. Upload your render and use **Magic Grab** on each tier, one at a time, to lift it out.
3. Imagine three equal boxes, each 512 wide: x 0–512, 512–1024, 1024–1536. Place Tier 1 in the left box, Tier 2 in the middle box and Tier 3 in the right box, each centred.
4. Leave clear black space between the tiers. Floating crystals must stay inside their own box.
5. Download as a full-size PNG.

## Job 3 (only when Rudy asks): recolouring small parts
For example: "the crystals are cyan but should be purple".
1. Click the picture → **Edit** → **Magic Edit**.
2. Brush over ONLY the wrong-coloured parts. Use several small brushes, one group of crystals at a time.
3. Type exactly what they should be, e.g. `glowing purple crystal shards, same shape`.
4. Pick the result that changes the colour without changing the shape.

## Checklist before sending anything made in Canva
- [ ] I only edited my own render; no Canva-made art.
- [ ] The background is pure black, with no grey smudges.
- [ ] Nothing new was added: no text, stickers, shadows or filters.
- [ ] It's a full-size PNG, named with `-canva` at the end.
- [ ] The three tiers still have the same shape, with black space between them.
