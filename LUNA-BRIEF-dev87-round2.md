# Luna brief: dev87 art, round 2 (fixes)

Thank you Luna, a lot of this is great and going straight in: the power card
pictures, the four gems, the bolt impact spark, the Nova burst, the second Hive Zone A
background (the calm dark one with the glowing domes at the bottom), the new chest
(empty body, closed chest, light burst), the drone thruster, the bug wings, sacs and
claws, and the chunky arm and leg parts. Below is only what needs another pass.

Same rules as always: plain pure black background, NO frames, borders, tiles or
panels behind the object, nothing cut off at the edge, one object per cell.

## 1. Bug bodies seen from ABOVE (most important)
The new bug bodies are seen from the side (legs walking on the ground). In the game
the bugs fly down the screen toward the base, seen from above, with a wing on each
side that the game flaps. Please repaint the five bodies seen from directly above,
head pointing DOWN, legs tucked under the body, NO wings attached (your new wings are
perfect, the game adds them):
- Scout (small, light), Drifter (wider, floaty), Spitter (with its purple goo sac on
  the back end), Diver (long, dart-shaped), Hive Mother (big, armoured, room for the
  sacs on her back and the two claws at the front).
- 512 x 512 each (Hive Mother 1024 x 1024), same gold armour and cyan/purple dots.

## 2. Locked socket cover
The game draws the base from the front and a little above, so each socket is a flat
oval about three times wider than tall. The new sheet is seen from straight above
and includes a square deck plate around each hatch, so it can't sit on the base.
Please paint ONLY the round hatch (no plate around it), squashed to that same flat
oval angle as the sockets on your base strip, 512 x 256 cells: closed (small lock
light), half open, fully open (folded down flat behind).

## 3. Small UI icons without their tiles
The coin, crystal, energy bolt, heart, level badge and padlock are lovely but each
one sits on a framed square tile, so they can't be cut out. Please the same six
objects on plain black with no tile. Make the heart RED-PINK (the game's hearts are
red so they read at a glance).

## 4. The pilot's bolt and the Spitter goo
- Bolt: the new one is a lightning bolt with branches; at its real size (about 14
  pixels long) the branches turn into noise and it looks like the Storm turret. Please
  a clean energy bolt instead: a short capsule of cyan light with a white-hot core and
  a soft tail, pointing RIGHT, 512 x 128, no branches.
- Goo: the glob looks like a little slime creature (round bumps like eyes). Please a
  flying glob instead: a teardrop of purple goo stretched along its path, pointing
  DOWN, with two or three small drips trailing behind, 256 x 256.

## 5. Patrol drone with real propellers
The patrol drone body is a small ship with fins and nowhere for a propeller. Please a
small quad-drone seen from the front and a little above: a compact body with 2 or 4
short arms, each ending in an EMPTY round rotor ring (the game spins the blades in
the rings). Same style as the propeller blade you sent. 512 x 512.

## 6. Chest lid on its own
For the opening animation: the lid of the NEW chest by itself, in its closed
position and size, on black (so the game can lift it off the empty body). 512 x 512,
same framing as the empty body cell.

## 7. More battle backgrounds in the calm style
The twelve world paintings are beautiful, but most are bright in the middle (desert,
ice, ocean, toxic, crystal, floating islands, jungle), which is exactly where the bugs
and bolts fly, so they would be hard to see. The ones that work best are dark and
calm in the middle with the interesting part at the top and bottom: your second Hive
Zone A, the space nebula, the neon city, the moon world. For each area please:
- Keep the middle 60% of the picture dark and quiet (no bright clouds, no light rays,
  no big objects there).
- Put the planet's surface and its landmarks in the bottom quarter, and keep the very
  bottom 12% simple: the game's base strip covers it.
- 1152 x 2048, one finished painting is fine (separate layers are a bonus, not a must).
Areas in order: 1 Hive world, Zone B "descent" (closer, glowing swamps, spores);
2 Scrap belt (wrecked ships and junk rings); 3 Magma (your lava world, darker sky);
4 Crystal (your crystal world, darker sky); 5 Plasma storm (your storm world, lightning
only near the top); 6 Sentinel machine world (dark grids, teal lights).

Not needed: the gold treasure chest and the cracked egg in the leftovers sheet (they
don't match the new chest and the sac design). The top-down Spitter with the glob in
that sheet IS the right camera angle: use it as the model for item 1.
