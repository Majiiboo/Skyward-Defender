# Luna brief: the six Specialty weapons (8 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief stands on its own: you do not need any earlier brief to follow it.

## What these are for

Each of the six heroes has one special weapon of his own that fires by itself every few
seconds while he fights. Right now the game draws them as simple shapes. I need your pictures
to replace those shapes.

`specialty_today.png` in this folder shows all six as they look now, one row per hero, top to
bottom: Snow Globe, Dragon Breath, Tesla Coil, Orbital Strike, Rift, Glaive. Use it to see how
big each one is on screen and what it does. Do not copy the look: those are placeholders.

Dragon Breath already uses your fire, so it needs nothing. The other five need 8 pictures.

## Two kinds of picture

**LIGHT pictures (7 of the 8).** Painted as glowing light on pure black, exactly like the shot
pictures you made (the ice pellets, the fireballs, the void orb). The game adds them on top of
the fight as light, so black is see-through and only the bright parts show.

**One OBJECT picture (the coil).** A solid thing, like the weapons you painted. Also on pure
black, which I cut away.

## Rules for all 8

- 1024 x 1024, `.webp`, the exact file name given.
- Pure black background. No text, no frame, no hero, no bugs.
- One thing, centred, with black all round it.
- Seen from **straight above**, because the fight is seen from above. (The beam is the one
  exception; it says so below.)
- Bold and readable when shrunk small: these are shown at about a fifth of the screen's width.

## The 8 pictures

### The penguin: SNOW GLOBE (ice blue and white)
He lobs a glass ball of snow that bursts and freezes every bug where it lands.

1. **`spec_globe.webp`** (light) The globe in flight, seen from above: a round glass ball,
   pale ice-blue, with a swirl of snow inside and a bright highlight on the glass. A faint
   frosty glow round it. It fills about 60% of the picture.
2. **`spec_globe_burst.webp`** (light) The moment it bursts: a round ring of ice shards and
   snow flying outward in every direction from an empty middle, with long thin ice needles and
   small snowflakes between them. It fills about 90% of the picture.

### Sparks: TESLA COIL (electric green)
He tosses a small machine under the bugs. It stands there and zaps them with lightning.

3. **`spec_coil.webp`** (OBJECT) A small tesla coil seen from straight above: a round base made
   of rusty scrap with three stubby legs, a ring of copper wire wound on it, and a glowing
   green ball in the centre. Junkyard-built, bolts and mismatched plates, like Sparks' weapons.
   It fills about 60% of the picture. The game adds the lightning itself.

### Rook: ORBITAL STRIKE (bright cyan with a little purple)
He marks the toughest bug with a target, then a beam comes down from the sky onto it.

4. **`spec_mark.webp`** (light) The target marker, seen from above: a clean military reticle.
   A thin bright cyan ring, four short thick marks pointing inward, a smaller broken ring
   inside, a dot in the centre. Sharp and technical. It fills about 80% of the picture.
5. **`spec_beam.webp`** (light) The beam itself. **This one is seen from the side and runs
   straight up the picture from the bottom edge to the top edge**, like your stream pictures:
   a white-hot line up the middle, bright cyan round it, thin purple edges, small flecks of
   energy travelling along it. The game repeats it upward, so the top edge must join the bottom
   edge with no visible seam. It is about 300 wide, in the middle of the picture.
6. **`spec_beam_hit.webp`** (light) Where the beam hits, seen from above: a white centre, a
   thick cyan shock ring round it, and rays and small arcs of energy flying outward. It fills
   about 90% of the picture.

### Hollow: RIFT (violet)
He opens a hole in space that drags bugs toward it and swallows the weak ones.

7. **`spec_rift.webp`** (light) The rift, seen from above: a spiral. An empty black centre
   (black is see-through here, so the emptiness is simply left black), a thick bright violet
   ring round it, and three or four curved arms of purple light spiralling **inward** toward
   the centre, like a whirlpool or a galaxy. Thin wisps being pulled in at the outer edge. The
   game spins it slowly. It fills about 90% of the picture.

### Nyx: GLAIVE (blood red)
She throws a spinning blade that cuts through bugs and flies back to her.

8. **`spec_glaive.webp`** (light) The glaive, seen from above: a three-bladed throwing weapon,
   like a three-pointed star with curved, hooked blades, all curving the same way round. Each
   blade has a bright pale cutting edge on its outside and a crimson body. A small ring in the
   centre where it is held. Elegant and sharp, like her pistol. The game spins it fast. It fills
   about 75% of the picture.
