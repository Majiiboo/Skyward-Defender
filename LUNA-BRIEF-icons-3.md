# Luna brief: ICONS round 3 (fourteen material icons, ten card pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
The Workshop now has MATERIALS: six element essences, a weapon line of three parts and a
drone line of three, and the keys you already painted. Today they borrow the element badges
and the Alloy / Cell / Core icons, so two different things can look the same in a price. And
ten new POWER CARDS (six for the new turrets, four from the pilot's star map) borrow the
turret pictures and the stat icons. You paint the real ones.

## The style to match
- Materials: your 48-cell icon set (`icons/ic_alloy.png`, `ic_cell.png`, `ic_core.png`,
  `el_fire.png` and the others): one rendered 3D object, light from above and a little in
  front, cool rim light, floating on plain black, filling about 75% of the cell, no text.
- Cards: your card pictures `cards/pic_heavy_bolts.png`, `pic_sparks_weld.png`,
  `pic_turret_overclock.png`: one object on black, about 70% of the cell, no text.
Our own designs, never another game's. Files go in `art_src/icons3/`.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Two icons that read the same at 24 px: the essences must differ by SHAPE, not only colour.
- Text, numbers or arrows painted in.
- A card picture that touches the cell edge (the frame covers it).
