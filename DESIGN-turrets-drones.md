# Turrets and drones: rework, retune, new ideas (Rudy, 2026-11-05)

Why now: heroes brought elements, star grades, buying with coins + parts, HP and DEF. The
base and the sidekick follow the same rules so the game has ONE language.

## Turrets
- **Element.** Every turret shoots one element and uses the same weak / resist table as the
  heroes (x1.35 / x0.75 by planet). Sentry Energy, Lance Pylon Fire, Storm Spire Electric,
  Gravity Anchor Void, Swarm Hive Dark, Prism Tower Ice. "Strong vs traits" stays on top for
  when the enemy families arrive (armoured, shielded, swarm...).
- **Role.** Each turret is one job, said in two words on its tile: Striker (Sentry: steady
  single-target damage), Breaker (Lance: bosses and armour), Chain (Storm: crowds), Control
  (Gravity: groups bugs, slows the field), Swarm (Hive: many small targets), Support (Prism:
  slows what it hits). Pick four roles for the stage, not four guns.
- **Grade and buying.** Turrets are rated by star grade and BOUGHT like heroes: Sentry 1★
  free; Swarm 2★; Lance 2★; Storm 3★; Gravity 3★; Prism 4★. Price = coins + parts, or
  crystals; Gravity and Prism also need a stage cleared first. Pilot level no longer gates
  turrets. An old save keeps every turret its pilot level had unlocked.
- **Mounts** still open by stage (5, 15, 25, 40). Tracks (ATK / Rate / Range, Lv.1-20) stay.
- **Retune.** Target: turrets about 30% of the damage at matching levels (they were 15-25%),
  measured with the balance sim after the element change.
- **Later ideas.** Support turrets that do not shoot: a Repair Node (heals the shield), a
  Beacon (hero ATK +10% while it stands), a Mine Layer; one card per turret exists already;
  turret skins (queue item 19); turrets with a resting and a firing pose (Rudy's note).

## Drones
- **Element: its own, by body** (Rudy, second pass). Scout Energy (the neutral starter),
  Striker Fire, Warden Ice; later an Electric spark bot, a Void wisp, a Dark bat, bought by
  grade. The point: a drone you own can be strong on a planet none of your heroes are, so hero
  + drone is the pairing you choose (cover your weak side). Pods stay jobs; support pods deal
  no damage, so they have no element.
- **Pods are jobs.** Attack pods (Twin Blaster, Beam, Missiles) stay. New: Repair Pod (no
  guns: patches the base shield 1.5 a second while the fight is on), Magnet Pod (no guns:
  gathers gems and coins near the drone and brings them to the pilot). More later: a Mark
  Pod (marks a bug: the hero crits it), a Decoy (draws Spitter shots).
- **Bodies** stay (Scout, Striker, Warden); their unlocks stay on pilot level for now, the
  buying model follows once the turrets' version has been played.
- **Retune.** Target: drone about 10% of the damage; the Repair and Magnet pods trade that
  damage for safety or speed.

## Roster depth (Rudy)
Same rule as the heroes: over time at least two drones and two turrets per element, each a
different job or build (a fast Fire drone that shoots, a slow Fire drone that burns and
repairs), so nothing is "the Fire one you must use", and hero + drone + turrets balance each
other out. New bodies and turrets come with Luna's art and their own grade and price.

## Page changes
- Turrets page: each tile shows the element colour, grade stars and role; locked turrets
  show their price and an Unlock button (coins + parts, or crystals) like a hero.
- Drone page: the pod list gains Repair and Magnet; a line says "Element: the hero's".
- Home and battle: nothing new to learn; damage numbers already show the element colour.
