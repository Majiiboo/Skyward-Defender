# Luna brief: gear in the 3D look (detailed version)

Hi Luna! This brief is long on purpose: it says exactly what goes in every square, so nothing needs redoing. Please read the whole thing once before starting, and tick the checklist at the end for each sheet.

## The goal in one sentence
Re-render the six gear sets you designed in the same **3D-rendered look** as the pilot, turrets and drones. Same designs, same colours, only the rendering changes.

## Look at these before you start (your own work)
Keep these open next to you the whole time. This is the look we want:
1. `pilot_rig/` parts: the helmet, torso and chunky arms and legs.
2. `drone/body_striker.png`
3. `turrets/lance_head.png` and `turrets/prism_head.png`

Also open your painted gear sheets: they are the **designs** to keep (shapes, colours, orbs). Do NOT copy their painted style.

## What "3D look" means, point by point
1. **Materials look real:**
   - Armour is matte painted metal with small scratches.
   - Trim is brushed or polished metal.
   - Visors and domes are glass with a reflection highlight.
   - Grips are rubber.
2. **Light:**
   - The main light comes from above and slightly in front.
   - The bottom of each piece is darker.
   - A thin cool cyan rim light on the outer edges, like the pilot.
3. **Shadows:** soft shadows inside the shapes, under plates and in seams. No hard black outlines.
4. **Edges:** plates have bevels that catch the light, and there are panel lines, bolts and vents.
5. **Glow:** light lines and orbs are bright, glowing, and cast colour onto the metal right next to them.
6. **No brush strokes, no painted outlines, no flat colour fills.** If it looks like a painting, it's the wrong style.

## Sheet rules (every sheet)
- **Size:** 1536 × 3072 pixels.
- **Grid:** 3 columns × 6 rows. Each square is 512 × 512.
- **Background:** plain pure black. No gradient, no floor, no shadow on the ground, no grid lines.
- **One piece per square,** centred, filling about 70% of the square. Nothing touches or crosses the square's edge, and floating crystals stay inside too.
- **No text, labels or numbers anywhere.**
- **Columns:**
  - LEFT = Tier 1 (basic)
  - MIDDLE = Tier 2 (upgraded)
  - RIGHT = Tier 3 (best)
- **Rows, always in this order, top to bottom:**
  1. Weapon
  2. Helmet
  3. Chest
  4. Gauntlets
  5. Boots
  6. Jetpack
- **All 18 squares filled.** Never skip a square, and never put two kinds of piece in one row.

## Camera, the same for every piece
- **Weapons:** seen exactly from the side, pointing RIGHT (barrel on the right, grip bottom-left). Flat side-on, no tilt.
- **Helmet, chest, gauntlets, boots, jetpack:** a 3/4 view from the front-left, slightly from above. This is the same angle as the pilot parts sheet.
- **Pairs:** gauntlets and boots are a pair (left and right) side by side in one square. Jetpack is one piece.

## How the three tiers differ (every piece, every set)
The **shape stays the same** in all three tiers. Same silhouette, same orb shape, same weapon type. Only these things are added:
- **Tier 1:** the clean base design. Light lines are thin and soft.
- **Tier 2:**
  - The SAME piece, plus 2–3 extra armour plates bolted on.
  - Light lines are thicker and brighter.
  - The orb or core glows stronger.
- **Tier 3:**
  - The SAME piece as Tier 2, plus polished gold trim along the edges.
  - The orb or core is brightest, with a small glow halo.
  - 4–6 small floating crystal shards around the piece, glowing in the set's colour (they stay inside the square).

**Easy test:** if you cover the gold and the crystals, Tier 3 should look like Tier 2. If you cover the extra plates, Tier 2 should look like Tier 1.
