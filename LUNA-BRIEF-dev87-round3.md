# Luna brief: dev87 art, round 3 (last few pieces)

Thank you Luna, round 2 is a big step: going in now are the six new backgrounds (Hive
Zone B, Scrap belt, Magma, Crystal, Plasma storm, Sentinel), the organic 3D bugs seen
from above (Scout, Drifter, Spitter, Diver), the energy bolt and goo drop, the coin,
crystal, energy bolt and heart icons, the quad drone and the chest lid.

Same rules: plain pure black, no frames or plates behind, nothing cut off, one object
per cell, same glossy organic 3D style as your new bugs.

## 1. Hive Mother in pieces
The game animates her egg sacs (they swell, burst, refill) and her two claws (they
sway), so they must be separate from her body. Your new Hive Mother has them painted
on. Please, seen from above, head pointing down, same style as the new one:
- Her body with NO sacs and NO claws (1024 x 1024).
- Egg sacs full, and egg sacs empty/burst (512 x 512 each, same size and place).
- Left claw and right claw (512 x 512 each).

## 2. Wings to match the new bugs
The new bodies are glossy and realistic; the wings from round 1 are flatter and
cartoon-gold. One wing for each bug on its own, translucent amber with veins and a
few cyan dots, the wing's root at the LEFT end and the tip pointing RIGHT (the game
mirrors it for the other side and flaps it): Scout, Drifter, Spitter, Diver, Hive
Mother. 512 x 256 each (Hive Mother 1024 x 512).

## 3. Locked socket hatch, lid only
The new hatch is painted inside its own ring housing, and the three sockets are joined
on one plate, so it can't sit on top of the base's own sockets. Please only the round
hatch LID itself, in that same flat oval angle, three separate cells 512 x 256:
closed (small lock light), half open (tilted up), fully open (standing up behind).

## 4. Two icons in the game's colours
The level badge (cream wings) and the padlock (silver) look like they come from a
different game. Please both in dark charcoal armour with cyan and purple light lines,
like the base and the pilot. 256 x 256, plain black.

## 5. Optional: Scrap belt background, second try
The sky above the junk pile is empty and the pile sits on a grey band at the very
bottom. Please: a few wrecked ships and a faint ring of floating junk near the TOP,
the junk pile along the bottom without the grey band, middle still dark and calm.

Not needed (the game flaps the wings and turns the bugs itself): the flap-cycle
frames, the angle sheet, the side views and the wing-pose sheet. They're great as
reference, but whole-body frames would make the bugs flicker again.
