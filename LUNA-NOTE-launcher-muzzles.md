# Luna note: the launchers again, with the muzzle seen from the SIDE (5 pictures)

Hi Luna! This note stands on its own.

First: the five pistols and Rook's rifle are in the game and look great. Thank you.

The four launchers need one change, and Nyx's launcher did not arrive, so this note asks for
five pictures.

## What is off

Open `WRONG_mouth_from_above.webp` in this folder (your Rook launcher). The body is painted from
the side, which is right. But the muzzle at the top is painted as if we look **down into it**:
we see the whole round opening and the glow inside, like looking into a cup.

In the game the launcher lies on its side with the muzzle pointing right. Then that round
opening faces the player instead of facing where the shot goes, and the weapon reads as bent,
or like a vase.

The wide flare at the top adds to it: it makes the launcher look like a trumpet or a goblet.

## What I need instead

Open `RIGHT_mouth_from_the_side.webp` (the pistol you painted for Nyx). Look at
its muzzle at the top: we see it **flat from the side**. The opening is not visible at all. The
rim is just a straight edge across the top, like the top of a tin can seen at eye level.

For each launcher:

- **Same camera for the whole weapon, muzzle included: a flat side view.** We must NOT see into
  the barrel. No round or oval opening. The top of the weapon ends in a straight, level edge.
- The glow from inside may still show: as a little light spilling **up** over that top edge,
  and as a thin glowing band just under the rim. Not as a lit hole.
- **A straighter barrel.** The muzzle may be a little wider than the barrel, with a thick rim,
  but not flared out like a trumpet. Think of a thick pipe with a reinforced end.
- Everything else as you painted it: the same body, bands, shoulder rest, grip, colours and
  details. Those are all good.

## The rules, as before

- 1024 x 1024, plain black background, no hand, no hero, no shadow, no text.
- Upright: muzzle at the top, grip to the right.
- The launcher fills about 800 of the picture's height.
