## Rules for all 15 pictures

- 1024 x 1024, `.webp`, plain BLACK background. No hand, no hero, no shadow, no text.
- **Camera straight ABOVE the weapon, looking down on its TOP.** We see the top of the
  barrel, the top of the body, the sight or rail that runs along it. We do NOT see the
  trigger, the grip or the magazine hanging underneath: from above they are hidden under the
  weapon, or only their edges peek out at the sides.
- The muzzle (the end the shot comes out of) points to the TOP of the picture. The weapon is
  perfectly upright, not tilted.
- The weapon ALONE in the middle of the picture.
- **SIZE: do not enlarge it to fill the picture.** Paint it at the size it would be beside
  the hero in his fight picture (his head is about 320 wide there). The lengths are given
  below.
- From above a weapon is narrow; that is correct. Give it a clear shape anyway: a wider body,
  a narrower barrel, and something along the top (a sight, a rail, a row of lights, a coil)
  so it does not read as a plain stick.
- Symmetrical left to right, as far as the design allows.
- The same design as the hero's side-view weapon, turned so we look down on it. Our own
  designs; never another game's.
