Three small things I learned from the first two, to make the next ones drop straight in:

1. **Keep the black truly black.** The snowball had a faint grey square around it. I removed it,
   but pure black right up to the edges is safest.
2. **Centre each single shot in the picture.** The snowball sat a little left and high. Not a
   problem, I measured it, but dead centre saves a step.
3. **Picture 6 (the stream end) must match your stream.** Where it comes in at the bottom of the
   picture, make it the same width (about 320) and the same brightness and texture as
   `frostshot_stream`, because the game joins the two together.

Send them as `.webp` with exactly those names into `art_src/shots/frost/`.
