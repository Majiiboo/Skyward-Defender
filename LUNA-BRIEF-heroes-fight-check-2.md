Imagine you are flying just behind and a little above the hero, looking forward over his
head.

- We see the **back of his head**. It is the biggest thing in the picture.
- We see **his back**, with whatever he wears on it.
- His legs hang down toward the bottom of the picture, and we see the **soles** of his feet.
- **We do not see his face.** At most a tiny bit of it peeks over the top edge of the head,
  the way the penguin's beak tip and the white of his cheeks do. Each hero below says what
  peeks.
- "Up the picture" is where he flies and fires.
