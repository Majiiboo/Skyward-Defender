## What this is
The game is a mobile shooter. A small chibi armoured pilot flies above a domed base and shoots alien bugs. He wears gear in **6 slots**:
1. Weapon
2. Helmet
3. Chest
4. Gauntlets (both forearms and hands)
5. Boots (both)
6. Jetpack (on his back)

The gear comes in **sets**, and each set has its own colour and look.

Each piece is painted **3 times**, as 3 tiers that show its upgrade level:
- **Tier 1:** the basic piece.
- **Tier 2:** the SAME piece plus 2–3 extra armour plates, with thicker, brighter light lines.
- **Tier 3:** the SAME piece as Tier 2, plus polished gold trim on the edges, the strongest glow, and 4–6 small glowing crystal shards floating around it.

**The shape never changes between tiers.** If you cover Tier 3's gold and crystals, it should look like Tier 2. If you cover Tier 2's extra plates, it should look like Tier 1.

## The look (very important)
**3D rendered, like a modern game,** the same look as your last batch. NOT painted, no brush strokes, no drawn outlines.
- **Materials:**
  - Armour is matte metal with tiny scratches.
  - Trim is polished metal.
  - Glass has reflections.
- **Details:** bevelled edges, panel seams, small bolts and vents.
- **Light:** from above and slightly in front, with soft shadows in the seams and a thin cool rim light on the outer edges.
- **Glow:** light lines, cells and orbs really glow and tint the metal next to them.
- **Crystals (Tier 3):** glowing gem shards in the SET's colour (see each piece below).

## Image rules (every image)
- **ONE image per piece,** showing its 3 tiers in one row: LEFT = Tier 1, MIDDLE = Tier 2, RIGHT = Tier 3.
- **Size:** 1536 × 512 pixels. Imagine three invisible 512 × 512 squares; each tier sits in the middle of its own square.
- **Spacing:** each tier fills about 70% of its square (a weapon may be long, but still no more than about 80% of its square's width). **There must be a clear strip of pure black between the tiers, at least as wide as a finger on the picture. They must NOT overlap or touch.** Floating crystals and ice shards also stay inside their own tier's square.
- **Background:** pure black. No floor, no shadow on the ground, no text, no numbers.
- **Camera:**
  - Weapons: exactly side-on, pointing RIGHT (front end on the right, handle at the bottom-left).
  - Everything else: a 3/4 view from the front-left, slightly from above.
- **Jetpacks:** NEVER have shoulder straps, backpack straps or shoulder pads. A jetpack is an armoured box or plate that mounts on the back, with its engines at the BOTTOM.
