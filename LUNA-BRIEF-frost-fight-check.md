# Luna brief: Captain Frost FOR THE FIGHT, a style check (2 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
In the menus the heroes look great. In the FIGHT they are tiny (about as tall as a thumbnail)
and each one is a single flat picture, so nothing on them moves: Rudy says the penguin "looks
like a static image". Your enemies do not have that problem, because you paint them from above
in PIECES and the game moves the pieces.

So we are going to rebuild the hero for the fight the same way as your enemies: seen from
above, in pieces, and the game will make him lean, flap, kick back when he fires and trail his
legs. The penguin (Captain Frost) goes first. The other five heroes follow only when Rudy is
happy with him.

Your menu art of Frost STAYS. This is a second, small version of him used only in the fight.

**This brief is only a STYLE CHECK: two whole pictures, no pieces yet.** Rudy picks one of the
two on his phone, and only then do I write you the brief for the pieces.

## Who he is (open this first)
`reference.png` in this folder is your own Captain Frost. Keep everything that makes him him:
- The white space suit with thin ICE-BLUE glowing lines.
- The clear round bubble helmet with frost on the glass, and the penguin's head inside it:
  black on top, white face, a YELLOW beak.
- The grey ear pieces on the helmet, the square glowing light on his chest.
- The jetpack on his back with two icy blue jets.
- The frozen rifle with icicles on it.

Same glossy rendered 3D look as the reference. Our own design; never another game's.

## The big change: make him MORE CHIBI
Rudy finds him a little too long. For the fight he should be short, round and cute:
- The helmet is about as big as his whole body. Head to boots he is only about TWO helmets long.
- A short, fat, egg-shaped body.
- Short stubby legs with big round boots.
- Short, wide flippers.
At thumbnail size the helmet, the yellow beak and the two blue jets must still be readable.
Bold simple shapes; small details will vanish.

## Rules for BOTH pictures
- 1024 x 1024, plain BLACK background, him in the centre filling about 75% of the picture,
  nothing cut off, no ground, no shadow on the floor, no text.
- In the game he flies UP the screen and fires UP. So his beak and his rifle point to the TOP
  of the picture. (Your enemies point DOWN; he is the opposite.)
- Light from above, plus a cool blue rim light so the white suit has a clear edge on black.
- His arms and legs must NOT lie on top of his body. Keep a clear gap of black between each
  flipper and the body, and between the two legs, as far as the pose allows. Later each one
  becomes its own piece, and I can only cut them apart if they do not overlap.
- No fire trails or motion streaks. The two jets may glow a little; the game draws the flames.

## Picture A: "the swimmer" (`frostfight_check_a`)
He flies the way a penguin swims: lying flat on his belly, head first.

The camera is DIRECTLY ABOVE him, looking straight down at his back.

From the top of the picture to the bottom you should see:
1. The tip of the rifle, pointing up.
2. The bubble helmet seen from above. Through the glass: the black top of his head, and the
   yellow beak poking forward (up the picture). A little of the white face on each side of
   the beak if it shows.
3. His shoulders and his back, with the jetpack sitting in the middle of the back. We see the
   TOP of the jetpack. Its two jets point backwards (down the picture).
4. His two short legs side by side, trailing behind him (down the picture), boots last, soles
   facing away from us or slightly toward the bottom of the picture.

His flippers:
- The LEFT flipper is spread straight out to the side like a small wing, flat, palm down.
- The RIGHT flipper holds the rifle. The rifle lies along his right side, pointing straight up
  the picture, its tip reaching a little past the front of the helmet.

Think: a toy penguin astronaut lying on its tummy on a black table, photographed from the
ceiling.

## Picture B: "upright, from above" (`frostfight_check_b`)
The same chibi penguin, but STANDING UPRIGHT in the air (hovering on his jetpack), not lying
flat.

The camera is ABOVE and BEHIND him, tilted: about as if you stood on a chair behind a small
child and looked down at them. We look at the top and back of his helmet and at his back.

You should see:
1. The bubble helmet from above and behind, large, at the top-middle of the picture. Through
   the glass: the black top and back of his head, and the yellow beak tip poking forward past
   the head (up the picture).
2. Below the helmet: his shoulders and the jetpack on his back, seen from above and behind.
3. Below that, getting smaller because they are further from the camera: his short legs and
   big boots hanging down.

His flippers:
- The LEFT flipper is held out to the side, away from the body.
- The RIGHT flipper holds the rifle out in front of him, pointing forward and up the picture,
  so the rifle's tip shows above the helmet.

## What happens next (nothing to do yet)
Rudy picks A or B. Then you get a second brief asking for the winner in pieces at one size:
the body with the jetpack, the helmet and head, the left flipper, the right flipper, the two
legs, the rifle, and the jet flame. The game moves them:
- he leans into a turn and settles back,
- the rifle kicks back with every shot, harder for a big shot,
- his flippers work as he flies,
- his legs drag behind him when he moves forward and swing the other way when he backs up.
