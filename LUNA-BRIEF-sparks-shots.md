# Luna brief: what Sparks's weapons FIRE (9 pictures of LIGHTNING)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
Your nine ice pictures for the penguin and your fire pictures for Ember are in the game and
they look great (`ice_shots_in_game.png` and `fire_shots_in_game.png` in this folder show them
in a fight). Now the same nine for Sparks, the junkyard robot, in LIGHTNING.

Sparks has four weapons and each fires its own kind of shot:

| Weapon | What it fires |
|---|---|
| Pistol | small, quick SPARKS |
| Twin pistols | BALL LIGHTNING |
| Rifle | a steady CRACKLING ARC of electricity that jumps from bug to bug |
| Cannon | one big THUNDER BALL that explodes |

Today these are simple shapes drawn by the game's code (`shots_now.png` shows them). Rudy wants
your painting in their place.

The game moves, turns, stretches and scrolls your pictures; you only paint the pieces.

## Open these first (in this folder)
- `ice_shots_in_game.png`, `fire_shots_in_game.png` = your earlier shots in a fight: this is
  how the pieces get used.
- `frostshot_stream.webp`, `frostshot_snowball.webp`, `frostshot_burst.webp`,
  `frostshot_stream_end.webp` = four of your ice pictures. The new ones must be laid out EXACTLY
  the same way (same size, same place in the picture, same black background), only in LIGHTNING.
- `shots_now.png` = Sparks's four shots today as code shapes. Only to show what each piece is
  for; do not copy the look.
- `hero_reference.webp` and `hero_weapon_side.webp` = Sparks and the rifle, for the colours:
  bright electric green with a white core (the element colour is green, not blue).

## Rules for every picture
- 1024 x 1024, plain pure BLACK background, black right up to every edge. No hero, no weapon,
  no text, no frame.
- These are LIGHT: the game adds them on top of the dark sky, so black becomes see-through and
  bright becomes bright. Paint glowing LIGHTNING: white-hot cores, bright electric green bodies, thin darker green edges fading to black. Jagged and crackling. No dark outlines, no shadows, no grey
  smoke, nothing solid and dark (it would simply not show).
- Everything FLIES UPWARD: the front of a shot points to the TOP of the picture.
- One thing per picture, dead centre, with clear black space around it (except picture 5).
- The same look across all nine, so they read as one family. Our own design; never another
  game's.

## The nine pictures

### 1. `sparkshot_pellet` : one spark
A short jagged bolt of electricity, like a tiny lightning strike pointing up: a white core, green glow, one or two little side branches. About 400 tall and 130 wide.

### 2. `sparkshot_pellet_big` : the charged shot
Every few pistol shots is a stronger one. The same shot, but longer and fiercer:
a bigger lightning bolt with more branches and a bright white point. About 600 tall and 220 wide.

### 3. `sparkshot_ball` : one ball of lightning
A round ball of electricity: a white heart, green glow, short arcs crackling off its surface all round, a few loose sparks behind (below) it. Lumpy, not a perfect circle. About 420 across.

### 4. `sparkshot_boulder` : the cannon's shot
A big ball of thunder: a white-hot heart inside a cage of thick green lightning arcs that wrap round it. About 620 across. It must look HEAVY. The game spins it, so no clear top or
bottom; make it roughly round overall.

### 5. `sparkshot_stream` : the crackling arc (THE IMPORTANT ONE)
A strip that the game repeats end to end and scrolls, to make one long stream.
Think of the arc between two electric poles: a jagged white line of lightning running up the middle, green glow round it, smaller branches flicking off left and right and coming back. Jagged is good, as long as the top edge still joins the bottom edge.
- It runs straight up the MIDDLE of the picture, about 320 wide, soft on the left and right
  sides (it thins out to black).
- It must touch the TOP edge and the BOTTOM edge of the picture, and it must LOOP: the top
  edge has to join onto the bottom edge with no visible seam, because the game stacks copies
  of this picture one above the other. The easiest way: keep it the same width, brightness
  and texture all the way up, with no start and no end. Your `frostshot_stream` did this
  perfectly.
- No bright blob at either end. Even from top to bottom.

### 6. `sparkshot_stream_end` : where the stream ends
The stream only reaches a short way and then breaks up. Paint that end: it comes in from the
bottom of the picture about 320 wide, the same brightness and texture as picture 5, and
splits toward the top into several thin branches of lightning that fork and fade, fading to nothing. About 800 tall, 700 wide at its widest. (Like your
`frostshot_stream_end`.)

### 7. `sparkshot_bits` : four small pieces on one picture
Divide the picture into four equal squares (2 across, 2 down), one piece in the middle of
each square, each about 300 across:
- top left: a tiny forked lightning bolt
- top right: a small bright spark with a tail
- bottom left: a four-pointed spark (a glint of light)
- bottom right: a soft round puff of green glow
The game throws these around the shots and the hits.

### 8. `sparkshot_hit` : a small hit
What shows for a split second when a shot hits a bug, seen from straight above:
a small burst of electricity: a white flash with short jagged arcs shooting outward. About 600 across. Round, the same in every direction.

### 9. `sparkshot_burst` : the cannon shot's explosion
The cannon's shot bursts when it hits. Seen from straight above: a big round blast, about 900
across. A white centre, thick green lightning arcs shooting outward in every direction, sparks between them. Round, the same in every direction; the game grows it and fades it.
