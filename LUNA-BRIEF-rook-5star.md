# Luna brief: ROOK at five stars, in his own colours (a small redo)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What happened
Your Rook tier 3 set (the white-and-gold armour with the cape) is beautiful and it stays in
the game, but as a SKIN the player can unlock later, not as his evolution. Rudy's rule for
evolutions: a hero keeps his own theme and colours all the way up his stars and GAINS things
(plates, a cape, brighter lights); colour changes are skins. Your Cpt. Frost, Hollow, Nyx and
Ember sets already do exactly that. So Rook needs the same five-star look once more, in his
own palette.

- Changing the pose, the camera or the cape's shape: only the colours change.
- Gold left anywhere: gold reads as the ceremonial skin now.
- A different purple from his base suit's light lines (match the Commander turn frames).
