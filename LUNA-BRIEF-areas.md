# Luna brief: the planets after the Hive world

Hi Luna! The game is now six planets of 10 stages each: Hive World, Scrap Belt, Magma
World, Crystal World, Plasma Storm, Sentinel World. Your painted worlds are behind
every one of them. Right now every planet still uses the Hive bugs; each planet needs
its own enemy family so it feels new. Start with the Scrap Belt (planet 2).

Same rules as the Hive bugs (they turned out great):
- Glossy rendered 3D, seen from directly ABOVE, head / front pointing DOWN.
- Plain pure black background, one object per cell, nothing cut off.
- Body and moving parts as separate pieces (the game animates them).

## 1. Scrap Belt family (planet 2): salvage drones built by the bugs from junk
Rusty orange and steel grey with sparks of cyan, clearly "made from scrap".
Same five roles as the Hive bugs so the game logic carries over:

| Role | Name | Look | Pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swarm | Scrap Mite | small round drone, two little rotors | body + ONE rotor blade (512 x 512) |
| Tank | Hulk Drone | wide armoured box with a junk shell | body + ONE side thruster panel |
| Shooter | Rivet Gun | drone with a barrel underneath and a glowing ammo pod on its back | body |
| Kamikaze | Spike Dart | long pointed missile-drone with a red warning light | body + small tail flame |
| Boss | Salvage Queen | big crane-like machine with two grabber arms and a cargo hold on her back that opens to release Mites | body (1024), left arm, right arm, hold closed, hold open |

Body cells 512 x 512 (boss 1024 x 1024), arms 512 x 512.

## 2. A boss for each later planet (one at a time, after the Scrap Belt)
Magma: a lava beetle with a molten shell that cracks open. Crystal: a crystal mantis
with shard blades. Plasma: a storm ray (manta) crackling with lightning. Sentinel: the
Sentinel itself, a machine eye inside rotating rings. Same piece idea: body + 2-4
moving parts.

## 3. Commander portrait for the briefings
When you reach a new planet, a short radio message plays ("INCOMING TRANSMISSION").
Please the commander who sends it: a head-and-shoulders portrait, rendered 3D like the
pilot, a calm older officer in the same charcoal-and-cyan armour style, with a holo
screen feel (slight scanlines, cyan rim light). 512 x 512, plain black. One neutral
face, one urgent face (frowning, mid-speech).

Thank you!
