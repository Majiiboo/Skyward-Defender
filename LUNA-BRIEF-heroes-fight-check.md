# Luna brief: the other five heroes FOR THE FIGHT, a style check (5 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for

In the fight the heroes are tiny, about as tall as a thumbnail. The penguin (Captain Frost)
already has a special small version for the fight: you painted him short and round, and then
in separate pieces, and the game moves the pieces. He leans when he turns, his weapon kicks
when he fires, his legs trail behind him. Rudy says he is perfect.

The other five heroes are still one flat picture each in the fight, so nothing on them moves.
They need the same treatment.

**This brief is only a STYLE CHECK: one whole picture per hero, no pieces yet.** Rudy looks at
the five on his phone. Then I write you a second brief for the pieces of each one.

Your menu art of the heroes STAYS. These are second, small versions used only in the fight.

## The folder

- `STYLE_penguin.webp` = the fight penguin you painted, the one Rudy picked. **This is the
  camera, the pose, the proportions and the amount of detail to match.** Only the hero changes.
- `rook.webp`, `sparks.webp`, `hollow.webp`, `nyx.webp`, `ember.webp` = your own menu picture
  of each hero, for his colours, materials and everything that makes him him.

## What to copy from the penguin, for all five

Open `STYLE_penguin.webp` and look at it while you read this.

- **The same camera.** Exactly the angle of the penguin picture: we see the hero's face and
  front, he floats upright, and the whole picture is turned so that **up the picture is the
  direction he flies and fires**.
- **MORE CHIBI than the menu art.** The head (or helmet) is about as big as the whole body.
  Head to feet he is only about TWO heads tall. A short fat body, short stubby legs, big feet,
  short arms. Compare the penguin with a real astronaut: that much shorter and rounder.
- **The same pose.** His RIGHT hand (on the right side of the picture, as on the penguin) holds
  his weapon straight UP, the tip reaching a little above the top of his head. His LEFT arm is
  held straight out to the side.
- **Nothing overlaps.** A clear gap of black between the left arm and the body, between the two
  legs, and between the weapon and the head. Later every one of these becomes its own piece,
  and I can only cut them apart if they do not lie on top of each other. Wings, a tail and a
  cape must also stand clear of the arms and legs (each hero below says how).
- 1024 x 1024, plain BLACK background, the hero in the centre filling about 75% of the
  picture, nothing cut off. No ground, no shadow on the floor, no text.
- Light from above, and a rim light in the hero's own glow colour so a dark hero has a clear
  edge against the black. **This matters most for Rook, Hollow and Nyx, who are nearly black.**
- No fire trails, no motion streaks. Jets may glow a little; the game draws the flames.
- At thumbnail size the big shapes must still read: the head, the glow colour, the weapon.
  Bold simple shapes; small details will vanish.
- The same glossy rendered 3D look as the penguin. Our own designs; never another game's.

## The five pictures

### 1. `rookfight_whole.webp` : ROOK, the cadet (glow: bright cyan, trim: purple)
The young soldier in charcoal armour.
- Keep: the big round charcoal helmet with the wide cyan visor line across the face and the
  round ear piece; charcoal armour plates with thin purple glowing lines; the small jetpack on
  his back with its two short tubes showing behind his shoulders.
- Weapon in the right hand: his charcoal energy rifle with the cyan strip, pointing up.
- How he flies: on the jetpack. The two jetpack tubes must be visible, one behind each
  shoulder, pointing down, each with a small cyan glow at its end.
- Make the visor line thick and bright: at thumbnail size it is his face.

### 2. `sparksfight_whole.webp` : SPARKS, the junkyard robot (glow: electric yellow-green)
A robot built from scrap. He is already almost chibi; keep him round.
- Keep: the huge round rusty ball head with ONE big round green eye in the middle, the two
  round ear pieces, the little antenna with a green light on top; the small round body with a
  green light on the chest; patched plates, bolts, the zig-zag green cracks of light; thin
  robot arms and legs with chunky boots.
- Weapon in the right hand: his welder gun, pointing up. Its hose hangs from the gun down to
  his body in one clean curve on the RIGHT side of the picture, not crossing the legs.
- How he flies: on a single round thruster under his body, between the legs. Show it as a
  short fat nozzle with a green glow, pointing down. Legs apart, one each side of it.
- The antenna stands straight up from the top of the head, clear of the weapon.

### 3. `hollowfight_whole.webp` : HOLLOW, the empty suit (glow: violet)
A suit of dark armour with nobody inside, only violet light. He is a ghost.
- Keep: the dark hood over a dark helmet, the two glowing violet eyes inside the hood, dark
  armour with violet lines, the ragged black cloak, the pale smoke coming off him.
- Weapon in the right hand: his dark void gun with the violet glowing barrel, pointing up.
- How he flies: he has NO jetpack. He drifts. His short legs hang down, and under and behind
  them the cloak ends in three or four long ragged tatters that trail downward like the tail of
  a ghost. Keep the tatters BELOW the feet and between and beside the legs, never across the
  arms.
- Keep the smoke light and thin, close to his edges. No big cloud: the game adds its own.
- Give the hood and shoulders a clear violet rim, or he disappears on the dark sky.

### 4. `nyxfight_whole.webp` : NYX, the night huntress (glow: blood red)
A cat-eared huntress in black.
- Keep: the big round black helmet with two pointed cat ears on top and the red glowing
  visor shaped like a narrow smile; the sleek black suit with thin red glowing lines; the short
  black cape; the long cat tail.
- Weapon in the right hand: her long black rifle with the red glowing coil, pointing up,
  standing clear of the right ear.
- How she flies: on a small jetpack hidden under the cape; show only two small red glows
  below the cape's lower edge, one each side.
- The cape hangs straight down behind her and is NARROWER than her shoulders are wide plus her
  arms, so both arms are fully outside it.
- The tail comes out low on the LEFT side of the picture and curls outward and up in one
  clean "S", well away from the left leg and the left arm.
- A red rim light on the helmet, ears and shoulders.

### 5. `emberfight_whole.webp` : EMBER, the dragon-kin (glow: orange fire)
A small orange dragon. She has no helmet: her head is the big shape.
- Keep: the big orange scaly head with two large horns curving back, the row of small spikes
  on top, the big amber eyes, the small ear fins; the pale belly scales; the brown leather
  straps and belt; the clawed hands and feet; the wings; the tail.
- Weapon in the right hand: her brass rifle with the orange glow, pointing up, standing clear
  of the right horn.
- How she flies: with her wings. **Both wings are spread wide and flat, one to each side,
  fully open like a bat's**, starting from behind the shoulders. The LEFT arm is held out in
  front of the left wing but lower, so we see black between the arm and the wing's lower edge.
  The right wing shows behind the right arm and the rifle; that overlap is fine, because the
  wing is drawn behind.
- The tail comes down between and behind the legs and curls to the RIGHT at its end, below the
  feet. It must not cross a leg.
- No fire from her mouth.
