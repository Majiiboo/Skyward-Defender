# Luna brief: gear (new designs, tiers, and 3 part icons)

Hi Luna! Rudy wants the gear to have real variety: every piece a **different design and silhouette**, not the same piece in another colour. Colour shows which set a piece belongs to, never how strong it is.

Same rules as always:
- Plain black background, one piece per cell, nothing cropped, no text.
- Square cells, the same angle and framing as the gear sheet.
- The painted style of your Ion Core Plate tier test.

## 1. The six slots (what makes each piece different)

| Slot | What should change from design to design |
|---|---|
| **Weapon** | The kind of gun: rifle, handgun, laser, cannon, twin pistols... (list in section 2). |
| **Helmet** | The helmet shape AND its visor: one long slit, twin round lenses, a T-visor, a full glass dome, a mask with grille vents, a horned crest... |
| **Chest** | The armour plate AND its power orb. Each chest has its own orb **shape**: circle, triangle, hexagon, diamond, star, crescent... set in its own socket design. |
| **Gauntlets** | Arm armour: slim bracers, heavy boxing-glove fists, clawed fingers, shield-plated forearms, cable-wrapped mech arms... |
| **Boots** | Leg armour: sleek sprinter boots, stompy mech feet, armoured greaves with knee spikes, hover boots with jets under the soles... |
| **Jetpack** | Silhouette and thrusters: twin round thrusters, a single big engine, wing-fins, a ring halo engine, four small rockets, a gravity disc... |

## 2. Weapon variety
Each weapon is its own design, painted side-on and pointing RIGHT:
- **Assault rifle:** long barrel and a scope.
- **Heavy handgun:** a chunky single pistol.
- **Twin pistols:** a matched pair, painted together in one cell.
- **Laser rifle:** a glowing emitter barrel with cooling fins.
- **Plasma cannon:** big and short, with a wide glowing muzzle.
- **Railgun:** two long parallel rails with energy between them.
- **Shotgun / scatter blaster:** a wide multi-barrel muzzle.
- **SMG:** compact, with a drum magazine.

In the game, each weapon fires differently (single bolts, twin bolts, beams, heavy slow shots, spreads).

## 4. Upgrade tiers (every piece)
Once a piece's design is set, paint it 3 times so upgrades LOOK stronger:

| Tier | Levels | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Lv.1–3 | The base design. |
| **Tier 2** | Lv.4–7 | Extra armour plates and brighter light lines in the set's colour. |
| **Tier 3** | Lv.8–10 | Gold trim, a glowing core or orb, small floating energy shards. |

One sheet per set: 6 rows (Weapon, Helmet, Chest, Gauntlets, Boots, Jetpack) × 3 columns (Tier 1, 2, 3).

## 5. Three part icons (small, 256 × 256)
Higher upgrades need parts that drop in battle:
1. **Alloy Scrap** (common): a chunk of bent, cyan-lit metal plating with a bolt or two.
2. **Plasma Cell** (rare): a glass capsule with a glowing purple-blue plasma core and metal caps.
3. **Void Core** (epic): a dark crystal sphere in a gold cage, with purple light swirling inside.
