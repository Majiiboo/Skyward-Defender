# Luna brief: gear tiers (and 3 part icons)

Hi Luna! Rudy loved your Ion Core Plate test (plain, then plated, then gold trim with floating shards). Now every gear piece gets the same treatment, so an upgraded piece LOOKS stronger:

| Tier | Levels | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Lv.1–3 | As the pieces are now. |
| **Tier 2** | Lv.4–7 | Extra armour plates and brighter light lines in the set's colour. |
| **Tier 3** | Lv.8–10 | Gold trim, a glowing core and small floating energy shards. |

Same rules as always: plain black background, one piece per cell, nothing cropped, no text. Same angle and framing as the gear sheet, square cells. Use the painted style of your tier test.

## What to paint
That's 18 pieces × 3 tiers. Tier 1 can be a fresh paint of the current piece in the new style, so all three tiers of a piece match.

One sheet per set (6 rows: Weapon, Helmet, Chest, Gauntlets, Boots, Jetpack × 3 columns: Tier 1, 2, 3):

- **Ion set** (cyan): Ion Pistol, Ion Visor, Ion Core Plate (your test), Ion Gloves, Ion Boots, Ion Jet.
- **Scavenger set** (green, rough salvaged look): Dual Minis, Scav Helm, Scav Tri-Plate, Scav Grips, Scav Treads, Twin Burners.
- **Void set** (purple, sleek and dark): Pulse Cannon, Void Crest, Void Hex Plate, Void Fists, Void Striders, Void Ring.

Please start with the Ion set; the other two can follow.

## Also: 3 part icons (small, 256 × 256)
Higher upgrades now need parts that drop in battle:
1. **Alloy Scrap** (common): a chunk of bent cyan-lit metal plating with a bolt or two.
2. **Plasma Cell** (rare): a glass capsule with a glowing purple-blue plasma core and metal caps.
3. **Void Core** (epic): a dark crystal sphere in a gold cage, with purple light swirling inside.
