Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
The game has a Weapons screen with a big showcase box: the weapon the player has picked is
shown large, lying on its side, so people can admire it. Rudy loves your side-view ice rifle
there (`weapon_side.webp`, in this folder).

The penguin also carries a pistol and a launcher. You painted those from straight ABOVE for the
fight (`frostfight_top_pistol`, `frostfight_top_launcher`). From above they are narrow, so in
the showcase box they do not look as good as the rifle. I need the SAME two weapons painted from
the SIDE, in the same style as the rifle.

## Open these first (in this folder)
- `weapon_side.webp` = THE STYLE AND THE CAMERA TO MATCH. A full side view, standing upright,
  muzzle at the top. Same frozen pale metal, frost crust, icicles, 3D-rendered look.
- `frostfight_top_pistol.webp` and `frostfight_top_launcher.webp` = the two weapons as you
  painted them from above. The side views must be clearly the SAME two weapons: same muzzle
  ring, same glowing ice-blue lines, same frost and icicles.
- `showcase_in_game.png` = the showcase box in the game with the rifle in it.

## Rules for both pictures
- 1024 x 1024, plain BLACK background, no hand, no penguin, no shadow, no text.
- A flat SIDE view, exactly like `weapon_side`: we see the whole left side of the weapon.
- The weapon stands UPRIGHT: the muzzle (the end the shot comes out of) points to the TOP of
  the picture, the grip points to the RIGHT. That is how `weapon_side` is painted.
- The weapon alone, in the middle, filling most of the picture height (about 800 tall for the
  launcher, about 600 tall for the pistol).
- Sharp and detailed: this picture is shown big.
- Our own design; never another game's.

## Picture 1: his PISTOL from the side (`frostfight_side_pistol`)
A short, chunky ice pistol.
- From the top down: a short thick barrel ending in the round glowing ice-blue muzzle ring;
  a blocky body with a small sight on its back edge and one glowing ice-blue line along it;
  a thick grip sticking out to the right, sized for a penguin mitten, with a trigger guard.
- Frost crust on the body, a few short icicles hanging from the barrel and under the body.

## Picture 2: his LAUNCHER from the side (`frostfight_side_launcher`)
A big, heavy ice cannon that rides on his shoulder.
- From the top down: a wide open muzzle with a thick frosted rim, glowing ice-blue inside;
  a fat barrel like a drum with two or three glowing rings around it; a boxy back end with a
  vent or two small exhaust ports.
- On the right side: a shoulder rest (a curved pad) and a front grip, so it reads as carried
  on a shoulder. On the left edge, the carrying rail or sight that runs along its top.
- Thick frost and a few big icicles.
