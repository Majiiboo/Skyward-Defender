# Luna brief: what Captain Frost's weapons FIRE (9 pictures)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
The penguin now has four weapons, and each fires its own kind of shot:

| Weapon | What it fires |
|---|---|
| Pistol | small, quick ICE PELLETS |
| Twin pistols | SNOWBALLS |
| Rifle | a steady FREEZE STREAM, like a water gun that sprays ice |
| Cannon | one big BOULDER OF ICE that explodes |

Today these shots are simple shapes drawn by the game's code. They work, but they look plain:
the stream looks like a laser. Rudy wants your painting in their place. `shots_in_game.png` in
this folder shows the four as they are now (pistol, twins, rifle, cannon, left to right).

The game moves, turns, stretches and scrolls your pictures; you only paint the pieces.

## Open these first (in this folder)
- `shots_in_game.png` = the four shots today, in a fight. Only to show what each piece is for;
  do not copy the look.
- `frostfight_whole.webp` = the penguin, for his colours and for sizes.
- `weapon_side.webp` = his rifle: the ice style to match (frozen pale metal, frost, icicles).

## Rules for every picture
- 1024 x 1024, plain pure BLACK background. No penguin, no weapon, no text, no frame.
- These are LIGHT: the game adds them on top of the dark sky, so black becomes see-through and
  bright becomes bright. Paint glowing ice: white-blue cores, ice-blue edges, soft glow fading
  to black. No dark outlines, no shadows, nothing solid and dark.
- Everything FLIES UPWARD: the front of a shot points to the TOP of the picture.
- One thing per picture, in the middle, with clear black space around it (except picture 5).
- The same icy look across all nine, so they read as one family. Our own design; never
  another game's.

## The nine pictures

### 1. `frostshot_pellet` : one ice pellet
A small sharp shard of ice, like a short icicle or an arrowhead, point up. A bright white
core, ice-blue edges, a short misty tail fading downward. About 400 tall and 130 wide.

### 2. `frostshot_pellet_big` : the charged pellet
Every fifth pistol shot is a stronger one. The same shard, but longer and heavier with two or
three small side spikes and a brighter glow. About 600 tall and 220 wide.

### 3. `frostshot_snowball` : one snowball
A round packed snowball, about 420 across. Lumpy, not a perfect circle. Lit from the upper
left, sparkling, with a few loose flakes coming off behind it (below it). No hard edge.

### 4. `frostshot_boulder` : the cannon's shot
A big jagged block of ice, about 620 across: sharp facets like a rough-cut crystal, bright
white-blue inside, frost mist around it. It must look HEAVY. The game spins it, so no clear
top or bottom; make it roughly round overall.

### 5. `frostshot_stream` : the freeze stream (THE IMPORTANT ONE)
A strip of rushing ice spray that the game repeats end to end and scrolls, to make one long
stream. Think of the water from a fire hose, but freezing: rushing white-blue mist with
streaks, ice crystals and small shards carried along in it.
- It runs straight up the MIDDLE of the picture, about 320 wide, soft on the left and right
  sides (the mist thins out to black).
- It must touch the TOP edge and the BOTTOM edge of the picture, and it must LOOP: the top
  edge has to join onto the bottom edge with no visible seam, because the game stacks copies
  of this picture one above the other. The easiest way: keep the spray the same width,
  brightness and texture all the way up, with no start and no end.
- No bright blob at either end. Even from top to bottom.

### 6. `frostshot_stream_end` : where the stream ends
The stream only reaches a short way and then breaks up. Paint that end: the spray comes in
from the bottom of the picture about 320 wide and fans out toward the top into a cloud of
frost mist and scattering flakes, fading to nothing. About 800 tall, 700 wide at its widest.

### 7. `frostshot_bits` : four small pieces on one picture
Divide the picture into four equal squares (2 across, 2 down), one piece in the middle of
each square, each about 300 across:
- top left: a six-pointed snowflake
- top right: a small sharp ice chip
- bottom left: a four-pointed sparkle (a glint of light)
- bottom right: a soft round puff of frost mist
The game throws these around the shots and the hits.

### 8. `frostshot_hit` : a small hit
What shows for a split second when a pellet, a snowball or the stream hits a bug: a small
burst of frost seen from straight above, a round splash of mist with a few short shards
flying outward. About 600 across. Round, the same in every direction.

### 9. `frostshot_burst` : the boulder's explosion
The cannon's boulder shatters when it hits. Seen from straight above: a big round burst,
about 900 across. A bright white centre, a ring of ice shards flying outward in every
direction, frost mist between them. Round, the same in every direction; the game grows it
and fades it.
