# Luna brief: what Nyx's weapons FIRE (9 pictures of SHADOW LIGHT)

Hi Luna! This brief has everything you need; no earlier brief or message is required.

## What this is for
Your nine ice pictures for the penguin and your fire pictures for Ember are in the game and
they look great (`ice_shots_in_game.png` and `fire_shots_in_game.png` in this folder show them
in a fight). Now the same nine for Nyx, the night huntress, in SHADOW LIGHT.

Nyx has four weapons and each fires its own kind of shot:

| Weapon | What it fires |
|---|---|
| Pistol | small, quick THROWING BLADES |
| Twin pistols | TWIN CRESCENTS, spinning curved blades |
| Rifle | a steady SHADOW RIBBON, like a silk ribbon of red light |
| Cannon | one DREAD ORB, a spiked ball that explodes |

Today these are simple shapes drawn by the game's code (`shots_now.png` shows them). Rudy wants
your painting in their place.

The game moves, turns, stretches and scrolls your pictures; you only paint the pieces.

## Open these first (in this folder)
- `ice_shots_in_game.png`, `fire_shots_in_game.png` = your earlier shots in a fight: this is
  how the pieces get used.
- `frostshot_stream.webp`, `frostshot_snowball.webp`, `frostshot_burst.webp`,
  `frostshot_stream_end.webp` = four of your ice pictures. The new ones must be laid out EXACTLY
  the same way (same size, same place in the picture, same black background), only in SHADOW LIGHT.
- `shots_now.png` = Nyx's four shots today as code shapes. Only to show what each piece is
  for; do not copy the look.
- `hero_reference.webp` and `hero_weapon_side.webp` = Nyx and the rifle, for the colours:
  blood red and crimson light with a pale pink-white edge.

## Rules for every picture
- 1024 x 1024, plain pure BLACK background, black right up to every edge. No hero, no weapon,
  no text, no frame.
- These are LIGHT: the game adds them on top of the dark sky, so black becomes see-through and
  bright becomes bright. Paint glowing SHADOW LIGHT: pale pink-white edges, bright crimson bodies, deep red fading to black. Sharp and elegant, like polished blades catching red light. No dark outlines, no shadows, no grey
  smoke, nothing solid and dark (it would simply not show).
- Everything FLIES UPWARD: the front of a shot points to the TOP of the picture.
- One thing per picture, dead centre, with clear black space around it (except picture 5).
- The same look across all nine, so they read as one family. Our own design; never another
  game's.

## The nine pictures

### 1. `nyxshot_pellet` : one throwing blade
A slim throwing blade seen flat, point up, like a narrow leaf: a bright pale edge, a crimson body, a thin red streak trailing below. About 400 tall and 130 wide.

### 2. `nyxshot_pellet_big` : the charged shot
Every few pistol shots is a stronger one. The same shot, but longer and fiercer:
a longer blade with two small barbs near its base and a brighter edge. About 600 tall and 220 wide.

### 3. `nyxshot_ball` : one crescent
A curved crescent blade, like a thin new moon, open side facing down, with a bright pale cutting edge on the outside and a faint red arc of light behind it. The game spins it. About 420 across.

### 4. `nyxshot_boulder` : the cannon's shot
A ball of crimson light with eight to ten sharp spikes pointing outward all round it, like a spiked mace head, with a pale hot centre. About 620 across. It must look HEAVY. The game spins it, so no clear top or
bottom; make it roughly round overall.

### 5. `nyxshot_stream` : the shadow ribbon (THE IMPORTANT ONE)
A strip that the game repeats end to end and scrolls, to make one long stream.
Think of a long silk ribbon made of red light: a smooth band up the middle with a bright pale line along one side, gentle folds and ripples in it, and a few tiny red motes drifting along it.
- It runs straight up the MIDDLE of the picture, about 320 wide, soft on the left and right
  sides (it thins out to black).
- It must touch the TOP edge and the BOTTOM edge of the picture, and it must LOOP: the top
  edge has to join onto the bottom edge with no visible seam, because the game stacks copies
  of this picture one above the other. The easiest way: keep it the same width, brightness
  and texture all the way up, with no start and no end. Your `frostshot_stream` did this
  perfectly.
- No bright blob at either end. Even from top to bottom.

### 6. `nyxshot_stream_end` : where the stream ends
The stream only reaches a short way and then breaks up. Paint that end: it comes in from the
bottom of the picture about 320 wide, the same brightness and texture as picture 5, and
splits toward the top into three or four thin ribbon tails that curl apart and fade, fading to nothing. About 800 tall, 700 wide at its widest. (Like your
`frostshot_stream_end`.)

### 7. `nyxshot_bits` : four small pieces on one picture
Divide the picture into four equal squares (2 across, 2 down), one piece in the middle of
each square, each about 300 across:
- top left: a tiny throwing blade
- top right: a small crescent
- bottom left: a four-pointed spark (a glint of light)
- bottom right: a soft round puff of red glow
The game throws these around the shots and the hits.

### 8. `nyxshot_hit` : a small hit
What shows for a split second when a shot hits a bug, seen from straight above:
a small slash mark: two thin crossed cuts of pale light with a crimson flash behind them. About 600 across. Round, the same in every direction.

### 9. `nyxshot_burst` : the cannon shot's explosion
The cannon's shot bursts when it hits. Seen from straight above: a big round blast, about 900
across. A pale centre, long thin blades of crimson light flying outward in every direction like a ring of slashes. Round, the same in every direction; the game grows it and fades it.
