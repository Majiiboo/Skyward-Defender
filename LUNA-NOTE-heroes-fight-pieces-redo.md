## What went wrong with the pieces

You were ahead of me: you made the pieces before I had written how. So the pieces were made
as new objects, and many of them are not the same thing as in the whole picture. When I put
them together I do not get the hero in the whole picture; I get a thinner, taller one holding
another gun.

The `WRONG_<hero>.png` pictures in this folder show it. In each one the top-left square is
your whole picture and the other squares are your pieces. Compare an arm, a leg or the gun
with the same part on the whole hero beside it.

Three things went wrong, again and again:

1. **Arms and legs are thin and long**, like a grown-up's. On the whole pictures they are
   short, fat and stubby, with big boots or big feet.
2. **The weapon is another weapon.** A different shape and design from the one the hero holds
   in the whole picture.
3. **A few pieces do not match each other** (Ember's two wings are two different wings).

## The one rule that fixes all of it

**Every piece is CUT OUT OF THE WHOLE PICTURE in your mind.** Open the hero's whole picture,
look at the part, and paint THAT part: the same shape, the same fatness, the same colours and
lights, the same angle, **the same size it has in the whole picture**. Not a new design of it.

The three `EXAMPLE_penguin_...` pictures show how it looked when it went right: the whole
penguin, his leg and his arm. See how the leg alone is just as short and fat as it is on him,
and just as small in its picture.

## Rules for all 29 pictures

- 1024 x 1024, `.webp`, plain BLACK background, no ground, no shadow, no text.
- The same camera as the whole picture. "Up the picture" is where the hero flies and fires.
- **Each piece at the same size it has in the whole picture.** Do not enlarge a small piece to
  fill its picture. A leg is small on the hero, so it is small in its own picture.
- **Each piece at the same angle it has in the whole picture.** The left arm sticks out to the
  side, so its piece lies sideways. The right arm reaches up, so its piece reaches up.
- Each piece ALONE in the middle of its picture. Nothing else in the picture.
- The same light as the whole picture, with the hero's own glow colour as a rim.
- "Left" and "right" always mean the left and right SIDE OF THE PICTURE as you look at it.
  On every hero the weapon is on the RIGHT and the arm held out is on the LEFT.
- Where a piece is hidden under another piece in the whole picture, **paint it anyway**,
  simple and rounded. The top of a leg is hidden under the body: paint the leg complete with a
  round top. When the leg swings, that part shows.
- The right hand is closed around NOTHING, like a fist with a hole. No weapon in it.
- The weapon alone has NO hand on it, and points straight UP the picture.

## How to check your own work

For each hero, lay the pieces on top of each other in their places: body, head, two arms, two
legs, weapon, and the extras. If the result looks like that hero's whole picture, with every
piece the right size and fatness, it is perfect.
